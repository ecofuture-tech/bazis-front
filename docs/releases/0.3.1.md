# bazis-front 0.3.1

Fixes of the components, of the generated screens and of the visual check, found by the live
runs of 0.3.0. It requires `bazis>=2.16.0`, as 0.3.0 did. `manage.py bazis_front update`
brings the components, the helpers of the tests, `playwright.config.ts` and
`vite.config.ts`; run `manage.py bazis_front screens` and `manage.py bazis_front e2e` after
it.

## The components and the screens

- **The related lists show their values as the list of their entity.** A related list of a
  card (`card.related`) gets the `display` and the `icon` of its entity, as the screen of
  the list does: a sum with the format and the currency of the field (`1200,00 ₽`), not
  `1200.00`. `RelatedList` takes `display` and `icon` and gives them to its `ResourceList`.
- **A card of a board wraps a long term.** The term and the value of a field share a line
  while they fit, the value goes under a long term instead of being cut to nothing (V017).
- **An item of a calendar shows its time to its end** (`10:00–11:30`) when its end is on the
  same day; in a day of a month, where it is narrow, its start, the range in its title.
- **The move of a card of a board says there is none.** The transits of a card are read when
  its menu opens; when there is none for the user, the menu says so, and the card hides the
  button once the menu is closed, while its status stays the same. A card whose menu was
  never opened still shows the button: the list does not say which items have a transit.
- **A link of the navigation after a group is set apart from it** by a line and a space, in
  the sidebar and in the top bar.
- **A column of the texts of a table keeps short words whole.** Its texts wrap within the
  same minimum and maximum width (`whitespace-normal wrap-anywhere min-w-32 max-w-32`, the
  first column `max-w-48`): a long text no longer narrows the column of a short address or
  an e-mail until its words break.

## The visual check

- **A screen is shot again once** when the proxy of the dev server did not reach the
  backend (a 500 `text/plain` without a body) or when the page loaded again between its
  check and its screenshot. `retried` of each result in `report.json` says so, and the
  summary of `manage.py bazis_front shots` counts the screens shot again.
- **V019** (error): the page loaded again between its check and its screenshot, also the
  second time. Such a screenshot was blank and the report clean before: the dev server of
  Vite reloads the pages once it has optimized a dependency that it found late.
- **The dev server of the tests optimizes the dependencies as it starts**
  (`npm run dev -- --force` in `playwright.config.ts`). With the cache of a previous server,
  a dependency that a screen imports since then (the charts of a dashboard, a board) was
  optimized once a page imported it, and the server reloaded the pages of the tests: a
  login that failed (V001), a blank screenshot. A running frontend of `E2E_BASE_URL` may
  still do so: V019 reports it. Its cache is its own, `node_modules/.vite-e2e`
  (`BAZIS_VITE_CACHE_DIR`, set by `playwright.config.ts` and read as `cacheDir` by
  `vite.config.ts`): `--force` deletes the cache that it optimizes, and with the cache of
  `npm run dev` the dev server of the developer running beside the tests lost its modules
  (404), while its own optimizations rewrote the modules of the pages of the tests.

## The data of the end-to-end tests

- **`e2e_data` only adds**, as the rules of bazis-mcp say: it never deletes a user or a
  record, neither those of the previous runs and of the scenarios nor its own. Run again,
  it finds its records by a key of their own, creates those that are missing, sets their
  dates relative to the day of the run and the password of the test users again. The
  sample creates its task once and moves its date (`sample/tasks/e2e.py`).
- **The scenarios pass again on the same database.** A generated test finds the items that
  its scenario created by their ids, never an item of a previous run with the same texts
  (the sample failed `manager-moves-a-task-on-the-board` the second time: it moved the task
  of the first run, already moved). `check` follows the values that a scenario fills in a
  create form (then in its edits); a `where` of `open_item`, `select` or `move` that has
  them is generated with `created: true`, and the helpers take only a row of the items that
  the submits of the test created (the answers 201 of the creates of the backend, their
  `data.id`). `openItem` also shows more items of the columns of a board (`action:more`)
  until it has the item: a column that grows with every run hid it.
- **A notification of a previous run does not count**: `expectNotification` takes the
  notifications that came since its test started, by their time (`<time datetime>` of the
  notification center).
- **P033** (warning): a scenario changes an item that it did not create (a transit, a move,
  an edit, a custom or a bulk action, a deletion, a related item added to it), a record of
  the test data that the other scenarios and the next runs expect as it is: it creates its
  item and changes that one. **P034** (warning): a scenario finds an item that it did not
  create by the texts of the items that a scenario creates. A count of a scenario is of the
  records of `e2e_data` alone (a filter or a title that no scenario gives its items).

## Not in this release

- **The sums and the averages of a dashboard** (a tile of the sum of `amount`, a chart of
  the averages). The core of Bazis has no aggregate of a field: the lists give their count
  (`meta=pagination`), not a sum, and a sum of the items loaded is wrong once they are
  paged. The key of the spec would be `tiles[].sum: <field>` (and `charts[].sum`,
  `charts[].avg`), validated as a number field of the entity that the role may view, and
  shown with the `display` of the field. It needs one of:
  - an aggregate endpoint of the route sets in the core (`/aggregate/?sum=amount` with the
    filters of the list and the permissions of the role), exported in the contract, a
    `useAggregate` and the key of the spec: about 2–3 days with the core and its release;
  - a custom action of the product (a `GET` that returns the sum) named by the spec
    (`tiles[].source: {action: <id>}`) and read by `useActionQuery`: about 1 day, but the
    contract does not describe the response of an action yet, so the value is not typed.
