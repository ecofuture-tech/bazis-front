# bazis-front 0.5.0

The data of the dashboards and of the boards in one request: the tiles and the charts read
the aggregates of the core, and sum, average or take the extreme of a field; the boards and
the cards read the transits that the backend gives with their lists and items. It requires
`bazis>=2.17.0` (the aggregates of the core) and, with the capability `statusy`,
`bazis-statusy>=2.13.0` (`for_transit`). `manage.py bazis_front update` brings the
components and the hooks; export the contract again and run `manage.py bazis_front screens`,
`e2e` and `shots` after it.

## What is new

- **The aggregates of a tile.** A tile of a dashboard counts the items of its entity as
  before, or shows the aggregate of its `value` over them: `{sum: <field>}`, `{avg: …}` of a
  number, `{min: …}`, `{max: …}` of a number or a date, formatted by the `display` of the
  field (a sum in a `currency`, a quantity in a `unit`, a date as a date). A DecimalField,
  a `string` with a `currency` or a `unit` display in the spec, is summed as a number.
  `StatCard` takes `value` and `display`; `tile:<id>` keeps the number in `data-value` (a
  date of `min` or `max` as its ISO text), and `expect: {tile: {<id>: 2.5}}` of a scenario
  takes any number.
- **The aggregates of a chart.** A chart counts the items per status or per period as
  before, or shows the `{sum: …}` or `{avg: …}` of a number field of its `value`.
  `StatusChart` and `TimeChart` take `value`.
- **One request a tile and a chart.** A tile is a list of one item with
  `meta=aggregates&aggregate=<terms>` (`count` without `value`), a chart one list with
  `group=status` or `group=<field>:day|month` over the range of its periods, instead of a
  count request per status or per period; a chart by week folds the days into the weeks of
  the language of the user. A 400 `ERR_AGGREGATE` of the core (too many groups, a field that
  the filter does not reach) is the error state of the part, with its message.
- **The aggregates in the client and the hooks.** `api.list` and `useList` take
  `aggregate` (the terms) and `group`; `aggregateValues(document)` and
  `aggregateGroups(document)` of `@/bazis/client` read `meta.aggregates` (the values by term
  without `group`, the groups `[{group, values}]` with it), typed `AggregatesMeta`.
- **The moves of a board from its lists.** With `move`, the lists of the columns ask for
  `meta=for_transit` of bazis-statusy 2.13: the ids of the transits each item may run now,
  in the same request. The menu `action:move` lists them at once (the payload of a transit
  from `TRANSITS` of the contract), a card without one has no menu and cannot be dragged,
  and a drop finds its transit without a request. `listedTransit(item, id)` of
  `transit-bar` is such a transit; the validators are not called before it runs: their
  refusal is the 422 of the transit, shown as `error:move`.
- **The transits of a card in its request.** `TransitBar` reads `state_actions` of the
  request of its item that `ResourceCard` makes: both ask for `ITEM_META` of
  `@/bazis/ui/resource` (`crud_actions`, `custom_actions`, `state_actions`), and
  `useTransits(path, id, meta)` shares the query of `useItem` with the same meta.
- **The sample** has the decimal `hours` of the tasks, the tile `review-hours` (their sum)
  and the chart `hours-by-month` of the home, and the scenario of the home expects the sum.

## The checks

- **S049** (error): the `value` of a tile or a chart aggregates a field of a type that its
  function does not take (a text, a relationship, the average of a date).
- **S050** (error): the `value` of a tile or a chart aggregates a field that the route set
  does not filter by (no `filter` in the contract): the core aggregates only the fields
  that the filter of the list reaches for the user.
- **C004**: the capability `statusy` needs bazis-statusy 2.13.0.

## Removed

- `useTransitsOf` of `@/bazis/react/statusy`: the transits of the items of a list are
  `meta.for_transit` of the list (`listedTransit` makes a `Transit` of one), those of an item
  `useTransits`.
- The menu of the moves of a board read when it opens, its loading and its text without a
  move (the message `board.no_moves`), and the button `action:move` hidden after it found
  none: a card has its moves with its list.
- The count requests of the tiles (`meta=pagination`) and of the charts (one per status or
  per period).

## Migrating from 0.4

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front 'bazis>=2.17.0' 'bazis-statusy>=2.13.0'   # bazis-statusy with the capability statusy
python manage.py bazis_front update
python manage.py bazis_front contract    # the OpenAPI of bazis 2.17: the parameters aggregate and group
python manage.py bazis_front check
python manage.py bazis_front screens
python manage.py bazis_front e2e
python manage.py bazis_front shots --no-run
cd frontend && npx tsc --noEmit && npm run lint && npm test && npm run build
```

1. `check` reports C004 until bazis-statusy is 2.13.0 or newer; pip refuses a bazis older
   than 2.17.0.
2. A product that used `useTransitsOf` reads `meta: ['for_transit']` of its list instead
   (`listedTransit(item, id)` for each id), or `useTransits` of the item.
3. A product that changed `resource-board`, `stat-card`, `charts`, `transit-bar` or
   `resource-card` merges them in `update`; a component of its own that counted with
   `meta: ['pagination']` keeps working, and may ask for the aggregates instead.
4. A message `board.no_moves` of the dictionaries of the product is no longer used.
