# bazis-front 0.11.0

What the passes of a product written with the specs only still spent on code of its own or on
the guides: the filter of a picker declared once for the entity, the meta of a file that an
action answers, the files of a public screen and a photo in a list, days of the browser in
the filters of a list and a tile, a boolean that a create form leaves out, and the lint of a
file of the specs as it is written. It requires `bazis>=2.17.0`, as 0.10 did; the format of
the contract is 12.

## Added

- **The filter of a field of an entity narrows its picker in every form** (`filter` of a field
  with `relation` in product.yaml: `{id: responsible, relation: user, filter: {roles__slug:
  receptionist}}`). The generator gives it to the create and update forms of the entity (of a
  list, a dashboard, a calendar, a form screen), to the edit of a card (`filters` of
  `ResourceCard`), to "add here" of a related section (`filters` of `RelatedAdd`), to a custom
  action whose `fields` name the field, and to a field of a payload of the same id and relation
  without a filter of its own. The filter of a form (`filters`, `filter` of a field of a
  payload) replaces it for its field. A filter of a field without `relation`, or of a file, is
  P002; its keys and values are checked as those of a form (S061). The hint of `filters` of a
  form that takes none (a create form of a dashboard, the add of a calendar, "add here") says to
  declare it on the field of the entity.
- **The meta of a file that an action answers** (`X-Bazis-Meta`): the JSON object of the header
  of its answer, declared in its OpenAPI (`file_meta=` of `http_post` of bazis 2.25, or the
  header in `responses=` before it), is `custom_actions[].file_meta` of the contract (its first
  2xx answer: the status of the route). The result of the form of the action shows its
  properties next to `{filename}` (`message: {en: 'Saved {filename}: {count} tasks.'}`, S053),
  `useActionFile` resolves to `{name, meta}` (`SavedFile`), `DownloadedFile` of the client has
  `meta` (`fileMeta`: null without the header, null with a warning when it is not a JSON
  object), and a scenario remembers them (`app.answer('count')`). The sample declares
  `ExportMeta` on `export`.
- **The files of a public screen** (bazis-uploadable 2.9): `anonymous_files` of the section
  `uploadable` of the contract, whether the guests read the files of the objects that a route
  set shows them (`anonymous_files()`; false before 2.9). A public screen that shows a file
  field while they do not is S062 (error): the route set must serve guests and declare
  `files_visible = ('<field>',)`.
- **`display: image`** of a `file` field (S020 of another type): `FieldValue` shows the file as
  its thumbnail only, a button that downloads it, 56 px in a list, a board or a dashboard and
  160 px in a card (`large` of `FieldValue`), a row of them for several files, an icon in place
  of a file that is not an image. The sample shows the photos of a task so on its card.
- **Days of the browser and lookups in the filter of a list and a tile.** A key is a field or a
  field compared by `__gt`, `__gte`, `__lt`, `__lte` (a date, a date-time or a number; S014
  otherwise, S008 of a field that the contract does not filter by); the value of a date or a
  date-time is `YYYY-MM-DD` or `today`, `month_start`, `month_end`, each with `±<n>` days
  (`month_end+1` is the first day of the next month; with ` HH:MM` for a date-time), computed by `relativeDay` of `@/bazis/react` (exported; the values of the
  fields of an action use it too) when the screen is shown: the filter of a list is a
  `useMemo` of the screen then, not a constant of the module. A day without a time is the
  midnight of a date-time, compared only by `__gte` and `__lt`: the equality, `__lte` and `__gt`
  of it are S014, whose hint gives the pairs `__gte: today`, `__lt: today+1` and
  `__gte: month_start`, `__lt: month_end+1` (or a day with its time, ` HH:MM`). `requires.filters` of the
  link of such a list names the fields of the keys.
- **S063 (warning): a create form with `fields` leaves out a boolean that the create requires**
  (`create_required` of the resources of the contract: the `required` of the request of the
  create route, a boolean without a default), which the form sends as false and the user never
  chooses.
- **`python -m bazis.contrib.front.spec.lint <file> [--json]`**: the YAML and the shape of a
  file of the specs (P001/P002, S001/S002, D001/D002/D003) with the hints of `check`, without
  Django, the settings or the database. `spec/lint.py` holds the schema layer that
  `spec/validate.py` used (`load`, `validator`, `schema_files`, the YAML and shape hints);
  `check` reports the same issues as before.

## Changed

- **The format of the contract is 12**: `file_meta` of the custom actions, `anonymous_files` of
  `uploadable`, `create_required` of the resources. `response` and `file` of a custom action
  are read from its first 2xx answer, not only from 200. Export the contract again.
- `saveFile` takes the name and the content of a file only; `useAnyActionFile` of the
  components resolves to `SavedFile`.
- `Backend.file` of the testing helpers of the components takes the other headers of the
  answer (`X-Bazis-Meta`).
- The test of the anonymous uploads (`tests/test_suggest.py`) requires exactly one code for a guest who
  reads back his upload, by the installed bazis-uploadable: 404 when `bazis.contrib.uploadable.routes`
  has `anonymous_files` (2.9), 401 otherwise (2.8).

## Dependencies

The thresholds stay `bazis>=2.17.0` and `bazis-uploadable>=2.8.0`: `file_meta` reads any
OpenAPI that declares the header (the sample declares it by hand and runs on bazis 2.24), and
`anonymous_files` is false before bazis-uploadable 2.9. Once bazis 2.25 and bazis-uploadable
2.9 are published, the extra `test` may take `bazis-uploadable>=2.9.0` (the sample serves its
photos to the guests) and the guide may name `file_meta=` as the only way; the minimum of the
capability `uploadable` (`capabilities.CAPABILITIES`) stays 2.7.0, since the section reads 2.9
only when it is there. A client of another origin reads `X-Bazis-Meta` only when CORS exposes
it (bazis 2.25 does).

## Upgrading

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # the client, the hooks, the components, the schemas of the specs
python manage.py bazis_front contract    # format 12
python manage.py bazis_front check       # S062, S063, S014/S008 of the lookups, S061 of a filter of an entity
python manage.py bazis_front screens
python manage.py bazis_front e2e
```

1. Code of the product that read `useActionFile(...).mutateAsync()` as the name reads `.name`.
2. The same `filters` repeated in the forms of an entity may become one `filter` of its field
   in product.yaml.
3. A calculated boolean field that only served a tile or a report (`is_today`, `is_this_month`)
   may become a filter by a day of the browser, `{date_from: today}`.
4. A public screen that shows a file with S062: let its route set serve guests and declare
   `files_visible` (bazis-uploadable 2.9), or leave the file out of the screen.
5. S014 is stricter. In 0.10 a filter of a date-time field by a string such as `'2026-10-31'` passed
   `check`; in 0.11 a day without a time (a day of the browser or `YYYY-MM-DD`) is accepted for a
   date-time only by `__gte` and `__lt`, and the equality, `__lte` and `__gt` of it are S014 (an
   error), since such a day is a midnight. Filter the date-times of a day by `<field>__gte: <day>` and
   `<field>__lt: <day>+1` (those of a month by `month_start` and `month_end+1`), or give the day its
   time, ` HH:MM`.
