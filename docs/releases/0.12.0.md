# bazis-front 0.12.0

The look of a product from its specs and its theme only: the fonts served by the frontend
itself, the cards of a list, the summary of a card, the brand and the sidebar on their own
tokens, the headings and the figures on the tokens of the fonts, the money and the dates in the
language of the product, and a filter of a tile by a set of values and by a negation. It
requires `bazis>=2.17.0`, as 0.11 did; the format of the contract stays 12.

## Added

- **Fonts served by the frontend.** A `fontFamily` token with `$extensions.bazis.font` names a
  starter of the package (`literata`, `golos-text`: the woff2 files of Fontsource 5.3.0 under
  the OFL, in the wheel) or an installed `@fontsource-variable` package (`package`), with its
  `axes` (`[opsz, wght]` for the optical sizes of a heading font), its `subsets` (`auto`: those
  of the `languages` of `product.yaml` and of the signs of `display.currency`), `preload` (the
  text font by default) and `fallback`, a local font with the metrics of the web font
  (`<Family> Fallback`; a starter has its own), so the swap moves no line. `bazis_front design`
  copies the files with `OFL.txt` into `frontend/public/fonts/<id>-<version>/` (commit them),
  writes their `@font-face` in `fonts.css` and `src/bazis/generated/fonts.ts`, the file that
  the plugin `bazisFonts()` of `vite.config.ts` preloads in `index.html`; the lock keeps their
  hashes, and `design --check` and `front.W005` report a missing or changed file. A font
  package is read from `frontend/node_modules`, which `design` needs; a check without it (a CI
  without `npm install`) compares the theme and the fonts with the hashes of the lock and
  reports a design changed since, while a new version of bazis-front or of the package shows
  only to `design --check` with the package installed. A token with `import` works as before.
- The starter layers `palette: pine` (spruce on linen, a clay brand, a dark sidebar) and
  `fonts: literata-golos` (Golos Text with headings in Literata and its optical sizes), for
  retail, travel, hospitality and the outdoors.
- **The mark of the brand**: `brand: {icon}` (lucide, D013) or `brand: {image}` (a file of
  `frontend/public/`) of `theme.yaml`, `THEME.brand`, next to the name of the product in the
  shell, on the new tokens `color.brand` and `color.brand-foreground` (the primary by default):
  an accent of the brand, also the rubric of a card, never a button or a badge. The name of the
  product takes two lines instead of being cut.
- **The sidebar on its own tokens**: inside `[data-slot='sidebar']` (the sidebar and the
  drawer of a phone) the components read `--sidebar-…` for the foreground, the muted text, the
  borders, the accent, the primary and the ring, so a palette with a dark sidebar stays
  legible; the new token `color.sidebar-muted-foreground`. D009 also checks the muted text of the
  sidebar on it, the foreground of its primary on the primary, the foreground of the brand on
  the brand and the brand on a card (a text accent: the rubric of a card). `color.sidebar-muted-foreground` is
  `var(--muted-foreground)` by default: a palette of your own with a dark `color.sidebar` and
  without it gets D009, as it should; define it (and `color.sidebar-primary`) for the sidebar.
- **The utilities `font-heading` and `font-subheading`** of the theme: the family, the weight
  and the tracking of a heading and of the heading of a section from the tokens, the new token
  `font.weight-subheading` (600). `alignsDigits` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`: the digits of a
  number, a decimal, a date and a date-time have one width (`tabular-nums`) in the cells, the
  values of a card and the inputs.
- The codes of the new checks: S064 (the cards of a list), S065 (the summary of a card), S066
  (a field both in the summary of a card and in a section of it), D014 (the heading font
  without its `opsz` axis), D015 (the `subsets` of a font without a language or a currency
  sign of the product), D016 (a `<Family> Fallback` in a stack that the generator does not
  make), D017 (`brand.image` that is not a file in `frontend/public/`).
- The cards of a list from its spec: `list.layout` and `list.layout_phone` (a table or a grid
  of cards, the composition of the theme by default) and `list.card`, the composition of a card
  of the grid, each role a column: `media`, the cover over the whole width at 4:3 (the first
  column with `display: image` by default; the icon of the entity without an image), `eyebrow`,
  a rubric above the title in `color.brand`, `title`, `figure`, the main value in the heading
  font, and `meta`, muted values, each after its label (`Залог 10 000 ₽`) (`ResourceList`
  `card`, `FieldValue` `media`, the thumbnail `cover`). Without `card` a card is as before.
  S064 reports a role that is not a column, a media that is not an image, a field in two roles
  and a list that never shows cards (a table on a wide screen and on a phone, by its layouts or
  else those of the composition of the theme).
- The summary of a card, `card.summary` (`ResourceCard` `summary`, `data-slot="card-summary"`),
  above the transits and the sections: `range`, two date or date-time fields shown as one range
  of the language with its number of days (`card.range_days` of the dictionaries, `{count} дн.`
  in Russian), `figure`, a number, a decimal or a date shown large, and `meta`, values under the
  range. Its fields need no section, and the edit of the card asks for them before those of the
  sections. S065 reports a range that is not two dates and a figure that is not a number or a
  date; S066 a field both in the summary and in a section.
- A set of values and a negation in a filter (`list.filter`, `filter` of a tile, and the new
  `filter` of the last items of a dashboard, `recent[].filter`): `{status: [issued, returned]}`
  is any of them (`Filter.or`), `{status: {not: cancelled}}` none of them (`Filter.not`), so a
  tile counts "not cancelled" or "in the revenue" without a calculated boolean field in the
  backend. A lookup (`__gte`...) takes one value (S014); an empty set and a negation of a
  negation are S002.
- `dashboard: {today: true}`: the day of today above the tiles in the language of the product
  (`today`); `dashboard: {links: false}`: no links that repeat the navigation (`links={[]}`).
- `display: {currency, fraction, per}`: `fraction: always` keeps the zero cents of a whole sum,
  `per` is the text after the sum and ` / ` (`3600 ₽ / сутки`), a text of the specs in each
  language (S027), given to the components by its key and shown smaller and muted after the sum
  (`data-slot="per"`), wherever the field is shown (a table of the staff too).
- `controls: {date: localized | native}` of `theme.yaml` (`THEME.controls.date`): the input of
  a date of `DatePicker`.

## Changed

- **A whole sum has no zero cents** (`fraction: auto`, the default): `1500 ₽` instead of
  `1500,00 ₽`, `15 000 ₽` instead of `15 000,00 ₽`; `250,50 ₽` stays. The tiles, the cells,
  the cards and the last items show it so. A scenario of your own that expects `,00` in
  `expect.values` (or a test of your own that reads the text) fails: expect the sum as it is
  shown, of four digits without a separator (`1500 ₽`), of five or more with a no-break space
  in each group (`15 000 ₽`), or give the field `fraction: always`.
- **The input of a date is a text in the format of the language of the product**
  (`DatePicker`, `controls.date: localized`, the default): `02.11.2026` in Russian,
  `11/02/2026` in English, typed in that order with any separator or as `2026-11-02`, with its
  calendar as before; it is marked `data-input="date"`, which `fill` of the end-to-end helpers
  reads, so the generated scenarios fill it with `YYYY-MM-DD` as before. A test of your own
  that finds the input by `input[type=date]` fails: find it by its `field:<name>`, or keep the
  input of the browser with `controls: {date: native}` in `theme.yaml`.
- **The headings and the figures of the components follow the tokens of the fonts**: the
  title of a screen, of a card, of a dashboard and of a card of a grid, the figures of a tile,
  of a card of a grid and of the summary of a card in `font-heading`, the headings of a section
  of a card, of a related list, of a form and of a calendar in `font-subheading`, instead of
  `font-semibold tracking-tight`. With a heading font other than the text font
  (`source-serif-display`, `ibm-plex`, `literata-golos`) the figures of the tiles and the
  headings of the sections are in it now.
- **`font.tracking-heading` is `-0.025em` by default** (it was `0em`), the `tracking-tight` that
  the components had. The starter pairs but `system` have a tracking of their own (`-0.01em` to
  `-0.025em`), which the headings of the components take now; with `fonts: system`, or a heading
  font of the product's own `tokens.json` without it, they look as before, and the bare
  `h1`–`h3` of the code of the product are a little tighter.
- The labels of a tile wrap between words, with hyphens in the language of the page.
- `init --preset portal` names the pair of fonts `literata-golos` (Golos Text with headings in
  Literata, served by the frontend with the optical sizes of the headings) instead of
  `source-serif-display`, whose headings D014 reports. Only a new product is concerned: the
  `theme.yaml` of a product is its own, and `fonts: source-serif-display` stays valid.
- `vite.config.ts` of the template imports `src/bazis/generated/fonts.ts` (`FONT_PRELOAD`):
  run `bazis_front design` after `update`, before `tsc` and `npm run build`.
- S014 also covers the filter of the last items of a dashboard and each value of a set and of a
  negation; a lookup takes one value. S020 also covers `fraction` and `per` without `currency`.

## Upgrading

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # the client, the hooks, the components, the template, the schemas of the specs
python manage.py bazis_front design      # fonts.ts, which vite.config.ts imports; the fonts of a starter in public/fonts/
python manage.py bazis_front check       # D014 on source-serif-display, S064-S066, D015-D017
python manage.py bazis_front screens
python manage.py bazis_front e2e
cd frontend && npm install && npx tsc --noEmit && npm run lint && npm test
```

1. `update` brings the components with their new classes: a copy that the product did not
   change is replaced; a copy it changed (`front.W007`) is merged, with a conflict where it
   changed the same lines (a heading of its own on `font-semibold tracking-tight`): take the
   line of bazis-front, then apply the change of the product to it again. `vite.config.ts`
   merges the plugin `bazisFonts()` the same way when the product changed its config.
2. A scenario of your own that expects `,00` in `expect.values`: expect the digits, or
   `fraction: always` on the field (above).
3. A test of your own that finds a date by `input[type=date]`: `field:<name>`, or
   `controls: {date: native}` (above).
4. A heading of your own on `font-semibold tracking-tight` may take `font-heading` (a section:
   `font-subheading`), so that the tokens of the fonts set it.
5. A portal made with `source-serif-display` takes the new pair by `fonts: literata-golos` and
   `design` (which copies its files to `frontend/public/fonts/`, committed with the product), or
   keeps its pair and the warning D014.
6. A calculated boolean field that only served a tile (`is_active`, `is_revenue`) may become a
   filter by a set or a negation, `{status: {not: cancelled}}`.
