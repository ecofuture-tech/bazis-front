# bazis-front 0.6.0

The form of a custom action as a page of the specs (the form of a guest without code of its
own), decimals typed with the comma of the language, the averages with the decimal places of
their field, the tiles of a sum on a phone, the menu of the links of the top bar and the login
of the screenshots. `manage.py bazis_front update` brings the components and the hooks; export
the contract again (format 9) and run `manage.py bazis_front screens`, `e2e` and `shots` after
it.

## What is new

- **The form of a custom action.** A screen `primitive: form` with `form.action` is the form
  of a custom action of the route set of its entity as a page (`ActionForm` of
  `@/bazis/ui/screens`): a sign-up, a request, the suggestion of a guest on a public screen
  (an action declared `public=True`), without a custom screen. Its fields are the properties
  of the body of the action read from the contract, never from a runtime schema (a guest may
  read none); `labels` labels them, `submit` its button. Done, it shows its `result`
  (`state:done`: a title, a message with `{the properties}` of the JSON answer of the action,
  the link to the screen `then` and `action:again`, which fills the form again), or a toast
  and the screen `then`. Its scenario expects `state: done`. The navigation links it for the
  users whose list of the entity lists the action (`requires: {path, can: 'action_<name>'}`
  of the generated link; a role whose list is refused gets no link), a public one at once.
- **The fields of the body of a custom action** (its dialog and its form, `ActionFields`): a
  property named as a relationship of the entity is the picker of its related items
  (`RelationPicker`, `RelationListPicker` for an array), searched by their search fields; a
  dialog also takes `labels`. Their titles: `labels` of the specs, which always win, then the
  field of the entity (of the runtime schema, else of the contract), then the property of the
  body.
- **Decimals in the forms, the payloads and the bodies.** A DecimalField of a form (the format
  `decimal` of the contract, which `ResourceForm` gives a field whose runtime schema has no
  format: `contractFormat` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`) and a `Decimal` of Pydantic in the
  payload of a transit or the body of a custom action (a number or a string in its JSON
  Schema, which `objectFields` now reads as a string of the format `decimal`, with the bounds
  of the number) are typed on one line (`DecimalInput`, `inputMode="decimal"`): `2,5` and
  `2.5` alike, the spaces between the thousands dropped, a letter not taken, sent as the
  string `"2.5"`. They were a text of several lines whose comma the backend refused (422).
- **The average of a tile** of a DecimalField is shown with the decimal places of the field
  (`places` of the contract): `1,55 h`, not `1,545 h`; `data-value` keeps the number of the
  backend.
- **A tile of a `value`** (a sum, an average, a minimum, a maximum) takes the whole row of a
  phone in `Dashboard`, so that a sum is not cut in a column of half its width; the counts
  keep half of it.
- **The top bar never widens the page.** `AppShell` with `navigation: topbar` shows in its row
  the links it has room for and the others, from the first that does not fit, in its menu
  `nav:more` (More, a dropdown menu used with the keys; a group with its heading), shown as the
  current link while the current link is in it. The row is measured in the browser
  (`ResizeObserver`) as the width, the fonts, the language and the links that the backend
  allows change; a phone keeps every link in its drawer. Eight links of a manager made a page
  of 1396 px at 1280 (V014 on every screenshot of a desktop).
- **The contract** (format 9) gives a decimal attribute `places`, the `decimal_places` of its
  `DecimalField`, and a custom action `response`, the JSON Schema of its JSON answer (null
  without one).
- **The sample**: the public form `suggest-task` of the guests runs `suggest` and shows the
  task it created (the scenario `visitor-suggests-a-task-on-its-page`); the create form and the card of a task have its `hours`, and the scenario
  `manager-sets-the-priority` types them with the decimal comma (`2,5`) and expects `2.5`.

## The checks

- **S052** (error): a property of the body of an action on a public screen is the picker of
  a relationship whose entity an anonymous role of the screen does not view (its `access`):
  the picker of a guest would be refused.
- **S053** (error): the message of the `result` of the form of an action shows a `{name}`
  that the JSON answer of the action does not have (`response` of the contract), or the
  action answers no JSON.
- **S054** (warning): a property of the body of an action or a field of the payload of a
  transition is of a type that the form asks for as JSON only (a union, an object, an array
  other than the ids of a to-many relationship).
- S032, S044 and S046 cover the form of an action (S044 no longer refuses it on a public
  screen); S030 lets the navigation link it and refuses a `requires` of its link (it requires
  its action); S006 refuses a screen of an item after it.
- **V020** of `shots` is reported only for a role without a login (nobody, an anonymous
  role) that the router sent to the login. A role with a `test_user` whose login fails is
  V001 with the error of its login, and a login that the proxy of the dev server did not
  carry to the backend is tried once again, as the other requests of a screenshot.

## Migrating from 0.5

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update
python manage.py bazis_front contract    # contract.json of format 9
python manage.py bazis_front check
python manage.py bazis_front screens
python manage.py bazis_front e2e
python manage.py bazis_front shots --no-run
cd frontend && npx tsc --noEmit && npm run lint && npm test && npm run build
```

1. `contract.json` is of format 9: `check` reports `C002` until it is exported again.
2. A product that changed `resource`, `resource-form`, `stat-card`, `dashboard`, `app-shell` or
   the helpers of the end-to-end tests merges them in `update`; `update` copies the
   `dropdown-menu` of shadcn/ui, which `app-shell` now requires. A component of its own that read
   `type: null` of a `Decimal` of a payload or of a body (`objectFields`) reads the format
   `decimal` instead.
3. An extension that gave a tile of a sum `col-span-2` on a phone may drop it.
4. A custom screen of a guest that only sent a custom action (its fields, its result) may
   become a screen `primitive: form` with `form.action`: delete `src/screens/<id>/` and its
   own end-to-end tests, remove `custom: true` and describe the form ("The form of a custom
   action" of the guide). Name the properties of the body as the fields of the entity: a
   relationship is then a picker, and a title is translated.
