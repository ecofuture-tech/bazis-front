# bazis-front 0.14.0

The state of an item and the permissions of the user in the screens: the tone of a calendar
by the status of its items, the actions of an item shown by its status (`when`), the tabs of a
card that the user may not view left out, a bulk transit on the rows that allow it; the
columns of a board that share its width, the numbers of a chart in the display of its field,
the toolbar of a calendar as that of a board, the decimals with their significant digits,
the shadows of every starter palette in its own hue; a guide read in fewer and larger
sections. The formats stay: the contract 12, `bazis-product/1`, `bazis-screen/1`,
`bazis-design/1` and the lock 1; every key of the specs is new and optional, and the specs of
0.13 are valid as they are. It requires `bazis>=2.17.0`, as 0.13 did.

## Added

- **`calendar.tone: status`**: every item of a calendar in the tone of its status
  (`statuses` of `theme.yaml`; a status without one is `neutral`), its plate and the dot of
  its day. An entity without a workflow is the error S071; give `label` the `status` too, not
  the color alone.
- **`when` of the actions of an item** (`when: {status: [...]}`, as that of a section of a
  card): the deletion and the update form of a card, a custom action of a card or of
  `list.row_actions`, and a custom bulk action are shown, and a bulk action runs, only on an
  item in one of these statuses. S072 reports a status that is not of the workflow, an entity
  without a workflow, or an action that is not of an item (a create form, an action of the
  toolbar of a list). It reflects the state of the item only: the code of the action refuses
  another state itself. In the components: `when` of `CardAction`, `RowAction`,
  `BulkAction`, `useCustomAction` and `useDestroyCardAction`, the type `StatusWhen` and
  `shownWhen(when, item)` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`.
- **The tabs of a card follow the permissions.** A tab of related sections only (`paths` of
  `CardTab`, which the generator gives such a tab) is left out, its trigger and its panel, for
  a user whom the backend lets view none of their lists; the first tab shown is open, and the
  tabs are loading until the backend tells. `useViewable(paths)` of `@/bazis/react` (and
  `useAnyViewable` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`): whether the user may view the items of each
  route set, as `useCan(path, 'view')` tells, with one hook for any number of them.
- **A bulk transit runs on the rows that have it**: `permission: {transit: <id>}` of a
  `BulkAction`, which the generator gives a bulk transit; the list asks for `for_transit` of
  its rows only then. One rule picks the rows of the page that a bulk action runs on: its
  `permission` (`for_change`, `for_delete`, `for_transit`) and its `when`; a selected row of
  another page is left to the backend.
- **The numbers of a chart in the `display` of the field of its value**: its figure, the
  difference, the total, the legend, the tooltips and the scale (from a million compact, with
  the currency or the unit; `per` not on the scale). The generator gives a chart with a `value`
  the `display` of its entity. A chart of a count stays plain.
- **`color.shade` in every starter palette**: a dark paper of the hue of its paper, in both
  modes, for the shadows of `surface: lifted` and the inner shadow of the tabs. A palette of
  the product's own keeps the default, the foreground.
- **The size of a column of a board**, `BOARD_COLUMN` of `@/bazis/ui/resource-board`: the
  columns of a board share its width, four beside the sidebar at 1280 px, from 14rem each
  (more scroll inside the board, each column snapped), at most 24rem.
- **`update` names the next steps**: after `npm install` when its `package.json` changed, `manage.py
  bazis_front status` and the subcommands that it lists (`contract`, `design`, `screens`,
  `e2e`, `shots`), then `tsc`, the lint and the tests; also with conflicts.

## Changed

The look of the existing screens changes in these places only:

| What | 0.13 | 0.14 | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| A decimal that is not a sum (without `display`, with `display: {unit}` or `plain`), also the average of a tile and `formatDecimal` in the code of the product | the digits after the point that the backend wrote: `1500,00`, `19,50 ч`, `512,500 ед.` | its significant digits: `1500`, `19,5 ч`, `512,5 ед.`, never rounded | at once |
| A sum (`display: {currency}`) of a code that `Intl` refuses, written after the number | the digits that the backend wrote: `12.5 euro` | with its cents, as any sum: `12.50 euro` (`250,50 ₽`, `3600 ₽` as before) | at once |
| The shadows of `surface: lifted` with a starter palette but `frost` | the foreground of the palette | a light tint of the palette in the light mode; the inner shadow of the tabs on the other surfaces too (it was light in the dark mode) | after `design` |
| The columns of a board | 18rem each: four scroll at 1280 px beside the sidebar | they share its width: four at 1280 px, at most 24rem | at once |
| The toolbar of a calendar | its filters in the line of its period, the reset there | the row of its filters on the plate of the theme over the navigation, with the reset among its tools (as on a board); the views and the add one group at the end of the navigation | at once |
| The numbers of a chart with a `value` of a field with `display` | plain numbers | in the display of the field | after `screens` |
| A tab of related lists only that the user may not view | «No access» in its panel | left out | after `screens` |
| A bulk transit | offered on every selected row, refused by the backend on those without it | runs on the rows that have it; disabled when none has | after `screens` |

The guide (`bazis/contrib/front/AGENTS.md`) names the sections that a product made from its
specs reads, and the others by when a step needs them; «The design» takes «Customizing the
look» and «The fonts and the brand» as its subsections; «The issues of the screens and the
design» has the codes `S…` and `D…`, «The issues of check» keeps `C…`, `P…` and the shape of
an issue; «Migrating the code of the product» is a section of its own after «Updating the
copies». The template `frontend/AGENTS.md` names them. The guide also tells the limit of the
groups of the aggregates of the core (`BAZIS_API_AGGREGATE_GROUPS_MAX`: a chart by day or by
week over many periods) and the rename of a `photo.png` of the product before the update to
0.10, which the rules of bazis-mcp 2.17 leave to it.

## Upgrading

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # the components, the template, the schemas of the specs
(cd frontend && npm install)             # when `update` says that its package.json changed
python manage.py bazis_front status      # then the subcommands that it lists
cd frontend && npx tsc --noEmit && npm run lint && npm test
npm run e2e                              # the expectations of the decimals (step 2)
```

1. `update` merges the copies as before (a copy that the product changed conflicts where 0.14
   changed the same lines: `resource-calendar.tsx`, `bulk.tsx`, `card-tabs.tsx`,
   `charts.tsx` and `resource-board.tsx` are the likely ones). The contract stays of format
   12: `contract` is needed only when `status` names it. `tsc` is green before `screens`: the
   props of the components only grow, and the screens generated by 0.13 work as before.
2. The end-to-end tests of the product that expect a decimal that is not a sum with the zeros
   at its end (`values`, a `where` of `open_item`, `select` or `move`, a test of `e2e/custom/`
   or of the product: `1500,00`, `19,50 ч`) no longer find its text: remove the zeros in the
   specs (then `e2e`) and in the tests of your own. `1500`, `19,5`, the sums and `fraction:
   always` are found as before.
3. `status` names `screens` when the product has a chart with a `value` of a field with
   `display`, a tab of related lists only, or a bulk transit; `e2e` and `shots` stay up to date
   until the specs change.
4. With a starter palette but `frost`, `design` is stale (`front.W005`): run it; the theme of
   `frost` stays as it is.
5. New errors of `check`: S071 (`tone: status` of an entity without a workflow) and S072 (a
   `when` of an action), only for specs that use the new keys.

## Fixed

- V009 counts the stretched `::before` of a control as its `::after`, and the phone of the
  screenshots is a touch screen whose `pointer-coarse:` styles apply.
- The contract tests of the date picker load its calendar before they wait for a day, so that
  a busy machine finds it in time.

## Known issues

- A bulk action whose rows of the page the backend does not allow stays shown, disabled:
  the permissions are known only by the rows of the page, and a button that left as the rows
  are checked would move the others under the pointer.
- No check of the specs tells a scenario that runs an action of an item outside its `when`:
  the end-to-end test fails, at the button that is not shown.
- The width of a column of a date, a date-time, a number or a badge is that of its values on
  the page, so V021 of a table a few pixels from the edge depends on the data (a two-digit
  hour): choose the `columns` of a list so that the longest date fits.
- The filters of a calendar wrap as those of a board and of a list, a whole filter at a time:
  with many at 1280 px they take a second line of their plate (no folded filters on a
  desktop).
- Left for 0.15: the lanes of a calendar and its view of a day; the tokens `size.base` and
  `size.target`; an endpoint of the permissions instead of the probe of `useCan`; a database of
  the end-to-end tests of its own; the widths of the columns from their types; `fields: line`
  or `box`; the order of the blocks of a card or a dashboard; the headings of the groups of the
  top bar; the number beside the title of a list with its noun; the initials of a name of one
  word; `when` of a bulk deletion and of a bulk transit.
