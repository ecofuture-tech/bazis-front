# bazis-front 0.13.0

Draft: the changes of the components, the focus of a dashboard, the avatars, the chips, the
header of a list and the sidebar (packages B, C, D and E of 0.13); the release completes it.

## Added

- **The focus of a dashboard**: `layout: focus` with `hero: {chart, side}` in a dashboard of the
  specs. The chart `hero.chart` (by a date) is large beside the tiles, which are the rows of one
  surface (a rail); the other charts are a column beside the last items under them, one column
  on a narrow screen. The chart shows the value of its current period (the last) large, its
  change on the period before and the figures of `side` beside it: `prev` the period before,
  `peak` the largest value, `total` the sum of the periods, from the values of the chart itself
  (no other query). In the code: `focus` of `Dashboard`, `hero` of `TimeChart` (the list of the
  figures) and of `Chart` (`{bucket, side}`), `layout="rail"` of `StatCard`; the texts
  `chart.hero.*` and `chart.side.*` of the dictionaries. S067 reports a focus without its chart
  or a chart in focus without the focus, a chart in focus that is not of the dashboard, is by
  the status or is not bars or a line, and the `total` of an average. A part that the user may
  not view leaves its place to the others of its row.
- **`kind: distribution`** of a chart by status: the sum of the values large, one stacked bar of
  their shares and their list with the value and the share of each (`value:<key>` with its
  number in `data-value`, as the legend of a ring), drawn without recharts. S068 reports a
  distribution by a date or of an average.
- **`display: avatar` and `display: chips`** of a field of an entity. An avatar is a name of a
  string after the circle of its initials, in one of the five tints `bg-avatar-<n>` chosen by
  the name; chips are the parts of a string separated by commas (a quantity `×N` in bold) or
  the related items of a to-many relationship, each a chip in `neutral-soft` cut with its
  title: two in a row of a list, then `+N` of the others, all in a card. The text of the value
  stays the name and the list (the initials and `+N` are drawn by CSS), so the scenarios and
  the titles read it as before. A heading of a card and an item of a calendar show them as a
  text (`inlineDisplay` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`), so does the summary of a card. A string with
  choices shows the label of its choice, one chip whatever its commas. S020 takes `avatar` of a string, `chips` of a string or
  a to-many relationship.
- **The header of a list**: `header: {search, count}` of a list screen. `search` puts the
  search, the view and the actions of the list in the header of the screen beside the title
  (`toolbar="header"` of `ResourceList`, `data-slot="screen-tools"`), one row with them on a
  phone; the filters and the sort of the cards stay over the list. `count` shows the number of
  the items of the search and the filters beside the title (`count`, `data-slot="screen-count"`
  with `data-value`), the toolbar staying over the list without `search`. S069 reports
  `header.search` without `list.search`. The helpers of the
  end-to-end tests search the list in the header too.
- **The number beside a link**: `count: {filter}` of a link of the navigation in product.yaml
  shows the number of the items of its list that the filter selects (with the filter of a
  report) at the end of the link, once the link is shown: `count` of `NavItem` (`{path,
  filter}`), `count:<screen>` with the number in `data-value`, nothing at 0 or before it is
  loaded; in the sidebar on the current link in `--sidebar-primary`, in the top bar and its
  menu on the muted background. The query is that of a tile counting the same items. S070
  reports a count of a screen that is not a list; its filter is checked as that of a list
  (S007, S008, S014). The sample counts the drafts beside the link of the tasks.
- **The plate of the user** at the foot of the sidebar and of the drawer of a phone, with his
  name (`user` of `ShellSession`): the circle of his initials in `--sidebar-primary`, his name,
  `detail` under it (new, optional: his role, his organization), the logout as an icon with its
  text for a screen reader, on the fill and the shadow of the current link (was a row: the
  circle in the soft primary, the name, the logout with its text; the top bar keeps it). The
  template passes no name: its session keeps the logout with its text.
- **The rail**: the last button of the tools of the sidebar (`Collapse the sidebar`) narrows it
  to 68 px, the icons of the links with their labels in their titles, a line for the heading of a
  group, the tools in a column, the circle of the user, a number a dot; `data-state="collapsed"`
  of the `aside` (`expanded` otherwise), kept in the storage of the browser (`bazis-sidebar`),
  never on a phone. The texts `shell.collapse` and `shell.expand` of the dictionaries.
- **The caption of a screen**: `caption` of `Screen`, a fact beside the title
  (`data-slot="screen-caption"`); `tools` and `caption` of `useScreenPage` are the places of the
  header that a part of the screen fills.

## Changed

The components take the tokens of the surfaces of 0.13 instead of listing their classes: a
block is `surface`, a lifted control `control`, the focus ring `ring-focus` of the theme. A
design without the new layers keeps the values of its tokens (the `:root` of 0.12 only gains
variables), and the look of the components changes as below for every product, whatever its
`surface`; `surface: lifted` (with `palette: frost`) lifts the same components without a
contour.

- **The fields are their bottom line** (an input, a select, a picker of a relationship, a text
  area, the input of a date): was a box with a border and the shadow `shadow-xs`, now a line
  in `color.field-line` (`-hover`, `-focus`, the shadow `shadow.field-focus` when focused),
  with no background; a disabled field is a dashed line (`disabled:border-dashed`, was the
  box at 50 % only). A filter and the range of two dates are one line under the label and
  their controls, the dates separated by a muted dash. A field of your own:
  `fieldClassName` of `@/components/ui/input`; a label with its controls
  `data-slot="field-group"` and `fieldGroupClassName`. The cross that clears the search is in
  the muted color of the text instead of that of the browser.
- **The buttons**: `outline`, `secondary` and `destructive` are a lifted control (`control`:
  `color.control`, `color.border-control`, the shadow `shadow.lift-05`, under the pointer
  `color.control-hover` and `shadow.lift-05-hover`). `secondary` was a muted fill: it is the
  outline button now. `destructive` was a red block: it is a control with red text, never a red
  block beside the main action. The primary button is a flat fill (under the pointer 6 %
  lighter, pressed 7 % darker, instead of 90 % opaque) with `shadow.lift-primary` (none
  without a layer). A disabled button has no shadow (`disabled:shadow-none`).
- **The toggles and the segments**: a toggle (`aria-pressed`: the chips of a filter of yes or
  no, the month and the week of a calendar) is a control, pressed in the soft tone of the
  primary (`bg-primary-soft text-primary-ink`, semibold); the chips were round with a primary
  border when pressed, the segments of the calendar were in a shared frame. The current tab of
  the default `TabsList` is on `color.control` with `shadow.lift-05` and a transparent border
  (was the background with `shadow-sm`).
- **The table reads in ink**: the header was on the muted background in the muted color of the
  text; it is on the card with a line under it (`--line-16`), its text the foreground at 75 %,
  semibold, the sorted column in `text-primary-ink`, the arrows of the sort shown under the
  pointer or the focus only (were at 40 % always). The cells after the first are the
  foreground at 80 % (were the muted color), the lines between the rows `--line-6`, a row under
  the pointer darker (the muted color at 80 % over the card). D009 checks the foreground at 75 %
  and 80 % on the card.
- **The status badge** is in small capitals: 11 px, semibold, uppercase, on the soft background
  of its tone (`variant="soft"` of `Badge`; was a rounded badge of a sentence).
- **The charts**: the scale shows 0 and the largest value only, the grid its zero line; the bars
  of a `TimeChart` before the current period (the last) are lighter (55 %), as those of a
  `Chart` with the new prop `periods` (values of your own stay alike); the title of a chart is
  in small capitals (`font-eyebrow`).
- **The blocks that were flat are lifted** by `shadow.lift-1` like the cards (`surface`): the
  result of an action and of a background task, the progress of a task, the grid and the list
  of the day of a calendar, its events (`shadow.lift-05`), the hero of a dashboard, a map and
  the thumbnails of the files. The summary of a card is one surface with a line between its
  parts (was the border color between cells 1 px apart).
- **The dialogs** are a surface on the card (were on the background), rounder (`rounded-2xl`),
  with `shadow.lift-2`; the menus, the popovers, the selects and the toasts have the border of
  the surfaces and `shadow.lift-2`.
- **One focus ring**: `ring-focus` of the theme (`shadow.ring-focus` over the shadow of the
  element, which stays) on every component: the buttons, the tabs, the badges, the checkboxes,
  the switches, the calendars, the links of the shell, the markers of a map, the close buttons
  of a dialog, a sheet and a toast (on the focus of the keyboard only, without the offset ring
  of shadcn/ui). A control in error keeps its red border, no red ring. A row of a table keeps
  its ring inside it (3 px of the ring at 50 %). What differs from the focus of 0.12:
  - the border of a button, a checkbox, a badge, a select and a tab is no longer in the color of
    the ring when focused (`focus-visible:border-ring` is gone): the ring alone shows the focus;
  - the solid rings of 0.12 are the ring of 3 px at 50 % of the theme: `ring-2 ring-ring` of the
    close button of a toast and of the thumbnail of a file (its ring under the pointer stays),
    `ring-[3px] ring-ring` of a marker of a map, `focus:ring-2` with its offset of the close
    button of a dialog and of a sheet;
  - a tab has no `focus-visible:outline-1` any more.
- **The tabs**: the track of the default `TabsList` is the muted background at 80 % with an
  inner shadow (was the muted background).
- **Under the pointer** a tile and a quick link of a dashboard and a card of a list that opens
  are lifted by `shadow.lift-2` without the border `border-primary/40` (was both, with
  `shadow-md`).
- **The tiles of a dashboard on a phone**: only the last of an odd number of tiles takes the
  whole row (was every tile with a value), so the row of a phone has no hole.
- **The last items of a dashboard** (`RecentList`): the title is in small capitals
  (`font-eyebrow`, was 14 px medium), a row is at least 56 px high with a line `--line-6`
  between the rows (was 44 px under a coarse pointer only, without a line).
- **The subtitle of a screen** is 12 px under its sticky header (was right under its line);
  the panel of the card beside a list leaves room for the shadows of its blocks at
  its right and at its end.
- **Inside the sidebar** the surfaces and the controls are computed on the colors of the
  sidebar (`--background` and `--card` are `--sidebar`): a card or an outline button that a
  product puts in `sidebar-tools` was white on a dark sidebar, it is on the sidebar now with its
  text. D009 checks the text of the sidebar on a control at rest and under the pointer and the
  focused line of a field on the sidebar (3:1). The avatars (`bg-avatar-<n>`) are colors of the
  content: not for the sidebar.
- **The links of the sidebar**: the current link was the accent of the sidebar with its icon in
  the primary; it is the fill `color.nav-active` with the bar `color.nav-active-bar` at its start
  (11 px from its top and its bottom, 2 px wide) and `shadow.nav-active`, its icon in the color
  of its text (with the defaults of the tokens: the accent of the sidebar without a bar or a
  shadow, as before but the icon). The icons have a stroke of 1.75 (was 2). The heading of a
  group was 12 px medium at 80 % of the foreground of the sidebar; it is in small capitals
  (`font-eyebrow`) in `--sidebar-muted-foreground`. A link after a group was set apart by a line;
  it is 12 px under it, without a line (a line remains in the top bar). The label of a link is in
  the title of the link (was of its text). D009 checks `--sidebar-muted-foreground` on
  `--nav-active` too (the detail on the plate of the user).
- **The day of today of a dashboard** (`today`): was a line of its own before the parts, it is
  the caption beside the title (under the title in a hero), once; a screen without a title has
  its header for it.
- **A number and its unit** (`display: {unit}`, a currency that `Intl` does not know): were
  separated by a space, now by a no-break space (`12 pcs` never split between two lines).
- **The calendars**: today in the calendar of a date picker was the accent (hardly seen in the
  dark mode), it is `bg-primary-soft text-primary-ink`; the days of the other months of a
  calendar of items were on the muted background (a lilac tint of `palette: frost`), they are
  on `neutral-soft`; the period above the grid was 18 px `font-subheading`, heavier than the
  title of the screen right above it, it is 16 px medium.
- **The filters on a phone**: the choice of a select, whose trigger takes the row, is at its end by
  the arrow, as the selects of a form (was after the label); the picker of a relationship is as
  narrow as its text, at the end of the row (was the rest of the row, its text at its start).
- **The first column of a table** was at least 12rem whatever its value: a number or a date
  there is as wide as it needs.
- **The sticky header of a screen** has the background only from `md`, where it sticks (was a
  band of the background on a phone too, over a page with its light).
- **The defaults of the controls in the dark mode** are those of shadcn/ui:
  `color.control` the input at 30 % (translucent), `color.control-hover` at 50 %,
  `color.border-control` the input (the border of a card in the light mode). D009 reads a
  translucent background over the page.

### With `surface: elevated` (a product of 0.12)

What differs from 0.12 besides the lines above, by the defaults of the tokens:

- a card (`Card` of shadcn/ui) has `shadow-xs` (was `shadow-sm`), as the other blocks;
- the blocks that were flat have `shadow-xs`; an outline button has `shadow-sm` under the
  pointer;
- a dialog, a toast and a submenu (`DropdownMenuSubContent`) have `shadow-md` (were
  `shadow-lg`);
- the current tab has `shadow-xs` (was `shadow-sm`) and no border in the dark mode (was the
  input);
- the line between the parts of the summary of a card is `--line-10` (was the border).

The colors, the borders and the radii at rest of a card, the summary of a card and an outline
button are those of 0.12 in the light mode, and so are their shadows, except that of a `Card`
of shadcn/ui: `shadow-xs` like the blocks of 0.12 with `shadow-xs` (a tile, a chart, the
summary), where it had `shadow-sm` (above). `assets/playwright/test/shell.spec.ts` compares
them with the classes of a block and of an outline button of 0.12. The focus ring of
`shadow.ring-focus` is that of most components of 0.12 (3 px of the ring at 50 %), with the
differences of the focus above.

## How to tune the new look

The components read tokens, so a product changes their look in `spec/design/tokens.json` (or
by the layers of `theme.yaml`), never in the components; `bazis_front design` writes them and
D009 checks the contrast of the colors.

- **The line of the fields**: `color.field-line` at rest, `color.field-line-hover` under the
  pointer, `color.field-line-focus` and the shadow `shadow.field-focus` when focused (the
  defaults: the input, the input, the ring and the ring of 3 px at 50 %; `surface: lifted`:
  the foreground at 32 % and 58 %, the primary and a line of 1 px of it).
- **The controls** (the outline, secondary and destructive buttons, the toggles):
  `color.control` and `color.control-hover` (their background), `color.border-control` (their
  border, `transparent` for no contour), the shadows `shadow.lift-05`, `shadow.lift-05-hover`
  and `shadow.lift-05-active`. The current tab has `color.control` and `shadow.lift-05` with a
  transparent border.
- **The blocks** (the cards, the tiles, the charts, the tables, the dialogs): the border
  `color.border-surface`, the shadows `shadow.lift-1` (at rest) and `shadow.lift-2` (under the
  pointer, the dialogs, the menus); `color.surface-light` gives them and the controls a
  gradient from it (none while no layer defines it); `color.shade` is the color of the shadows
  of `surface: lifted`.
- **The primary button**: `shadow.lift-primary`, `shadow.lift-primary-hover`,
  `shadow.lift-primary-active` (none by default).
- **The focus ring**: `shadow.ring-focus` (the ring of 3 px at 50 % by default; `surface:
  lifted`: 1 px of the ring with a halo of 4 px at 18 %).
- **The sidebar and the page**: the gradient of the sidebar `color.sidebar-to`, its light
  `color.sidebar-glow`; the light of the page `color.glow-a` and `color.glow-b` (none while no
  layer defines them; `palette: frost` does).
- **The status badge**: `StatusLabel` is `Badge` with `variant="soft"`; a product that wants
  the rounded badge of a sentence back composes its own label with another variant of `Badge`
  in the soft tone of the status (`SOFT_TONES`, `statusTone` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`).
