# bazis-front 0.13.0

The new look of a product: the cool paper `frost`, the one grotesque `golos` and the surfaces
`surface: lifted`, lifted by their shadows without a contour, which `init` gives a new
product; the components on the tokens of their surfaces, their controls and their focus; the
focus of a dashboard and a distribution of the statuses; names as avatars and parts as chips;
the search and the count of a list in the header of its screen; the number beside a link, the
plate of the user and the rail of the sidebar. The formats stay: the contract 12,
`bazis-product/1`, `bazis-screen/1`, `bazis-design/1` and the lock 1; every key of the specs
is new and optional. It requires `bazis>=2.17.0`, as 0.12 did.

## Added

- **The layers of the new look.** `palette: frost`: cool paper (its hue at least 60 from that
  of the accent, its chroma at most 0.008, so it reads as white), a cedar green primary and a
  honey brand, a deep cedar sidebar with its gradient and its glow, the light of the page and
  the shadows in the tone of a dark paper. `fonts: golos`: Golos Text alone for the text, the
  headings and the figures, served by the frontend itself (one variable file of every weight
  for each subset). `surface: lifted`: the blocks and the controls without a border, resting on
  the page by blurred shadows in heights, the fields a bottom line, the primary button with a
  shadow of its own color, one focus ring. They go together (the domains: rentals, bookings,
  team workspaces) and with every other layer: D009 checks every combination.
- **`init`** names `palette: frost`, `fonts: golos` and `surface: lifted` for both presets,
  with the shape of the preset: a workspace was `indigo`, `inter`, `soft`, `elevated`, a portal
  `ocean`, `literata-golos`, `round`, `elevated`. A new product serves Golos Text alone in
  `frontend/public/fonts/`; `ocean`, `literata-golos` and the other layers stay a choice, listed
  in the comments of the starters. The `theme.yaml` of an existing product is never rewritten.
- **The tokens of the surfaces** (the defaults are the look before them: a design without the
  new layers keeps the values of its tokens): `color.border-surface`, `color.border-control`,
  `color.control`, `color.control-hover`, `color.field-line` (`-hover`, `-focus`),
  `color.surface-light`, `color.shade`, `shadow.lift-05` (`-hover`, `-active`),
  `shadow.lift-1`, `shadow.lift-2`, `shadow.lift-primary` (`-hover`, `-active`),
  `shadow.ring-focus`, `shadow.field-focus`; of the sidebar and the page `color.sidebar-to`,
  `color.sidebar-glow`, `color.glow-a`, `color.glow-b` (not drawn while no layer defines them)
  and of its current link `color.nav-active`, `color.nav-active-bar`, `shadow.nav-active`; the
  derived `--line-6`, `--line-10`, `--line-16` (the lines between the rows and under a head) and
  the five tints of the avatars `--avatar-<n>` with their `-fg`, which D009 checks. The
  utilities of the theme `surface` (a lifted block), `control` (a lifted control), `ring-focus`
  (the focus ring over the shadow of the element) and `font-eyebrow` (small capitals).
- **The focus of a dashboard**: `layout: focus` with `hero: {chart, side}` in a dashboard of the
  specs. The chart `hero.chart` (by a date) is large beside the tiles, which are the rows of one
  surface (a rail); the other charts are a column beside the last items under them, one column
  on a narrow screen. The chart shows the value of its current period (the last) large, its
  change on the period before and the figures of `side` beside it: `prev` the period before,
  `peak` the largest value, `total` the sum of the periods, from the values of the chart itself
  (no other query). In the code: `focus` of `Dashboard`, `hero` of `TimeChart` (the list of the
  figures) and of `Chart` (`{bucket, side}`), `layout="rail"` of `StatCard`; the texts
  `chart.hero.*` and `chart.side.*` of the dictionaries. S067 reports a focus without its chart
  or a chart in focus without the focus, a chart in focus that is not of the dashboard, is by
  the status or is not bars or a line, and the `total` of an average. A part that the user may
  not view leaves its place to the others of its row.
- **`kind: distribution`** of a chart by status: the sum of the values large, one stacked bar of
  their shares and their list with the value and the share of each (`value:<key>` with its
  number in `data-value`, as the legend of a ring), drawn without recharts. S068 reports a
  distribution by a date or of an average.
- **`display: avatar` and `display: chips`** of a field of an entity. An avatar is a name of a
  string after the circle of its initials (one letter for a name of one word), in one of the
  five tints `bg-avatar-<n>` chosen by the name; chips are the parts of a string separated by
  commas (a quantity `×N` in bold) or the related items of a to-many relationship, each a chip
  in `neutral-soft` cut with its title: two in a row of a list, one in the last items of a
  dashboard, then `+N` of the others, all in a card. The text of the value stays the name and
  the list (the initials and `+N` are drawn by CSS), so the scenarios and the titles read it as
  before. A heading of a card and an item of a calendar show them as a text (`inlineDisplay` of
  `@/bazis/ui/resource`), so does the summary of a card. A string with choices shows the label
  of its choice, one chip whatever its commas. S020 takes `avatar` of a string, `chips` of a
  string or a to-many relationship.
- **The header of a list**: `header: {search, count}` of a list screen. `search` puts the
  search, the view and the actions of the list in the header of the screen beside the title
  (`toolbar="header"` of `ResourceList`, `data-slot="screen-tools"`), one row with them on a
  phone; the filters and the sort of the cards stay over the list. `count` shows the number of
  the items of the search and the filters beside the title (`count`, `data-slot="screen-count"`
  with `data-value`, the number alone: the specs have no plural of an entity), the toolbar
  staying over the list without `search`. S069 reports `header.search` without `list.search`.
  The helpers of the end-to-end tests search the list in the header too. `header.search: false`
  keeps them over the list; without `header.search` a list takes `composition.list_tools` of the
  theme (below).
- **The number beside a link**: `count: {filter}` of a link of the navigation in product.yaml
  shows the number of the items of its list that the filter selects (with the filter of a
  report) at the end of the link, once the link is shown: `count` of `NavItem` (`{path,
  filter}`), `count:<screen>` with the number in `data-value`; in the sidebar on the current
  link in `--sidebar-primary`, in the top bar and its menu on the muted background. The number
  is the aggregate `count` of the list (`meta: ['aggregates']`, `aggregate: ['count']`, one item
  a page), not `meta.count`: it is the query of a tile counting the same items, so a link and
  such a tile make one request.
  Nothing is shown before it is loaded, at 0, and when the request fails: the number hides
  without a message (the link stays), as a hint and not a state of a screen. S070 reports a
  count of a screen that is not a list; its filter is checked as that of a list (S007, S008,
  S014). The sample counts the drafts beside the link of the tasks.
- **The plate of the user** at the foot of the sidebar and of the drawer of a phone, with his
  name (`user` of `ShellSession`): the circle of his initials in `--sidebar-primary`, his name,
  `detail` under it (new, optional: his role, his organization), the logout as an icon with its
  text for a screen reader, on the fill and the shadow of the current link (was a row: the
  circle in the soft primary, the name, the logout with its text; the top bar keeps it). The
  template passes no name: its session keeps the logout with its text.
- **The rail**: the last button of the tools of the sidebar (`Collapse the sidebar`) narrows it
  to 68 px, the icons of the links with their labels in their titles, a line for the heading of a
  group, the tools in a column, the circle of the user, a number a dot; `data-state="collapsed"`
  of the `aside` (`expanded` otherwise), the choice of the user kept in the storage of the
  browser (`bazis-sidebar`: `collapsed` or `expanded`), never on a phone. The texts
  `shell.collapse` and `shell.expand` of the dictionaries. Every product with a sidebar has it:
  until the user chooses, the sidebar is expanded from `lg` and a rail below (`shell.sidebar:
  auto`), or expanded or a rail at every width (`expanded`, `rail`; below). The logout and the sign-in without the plate keep
  their names in their titles.
- **The keys of the base look** (new, optional, `bazis-design/1`): the look of `init` is the base,
  which a product moves and restyles without the copies of the components. `shell.sidebar`
  (`auto`, the default: expanded from `lg`, a rail below; `expanded` or `rail` at every width:
  the sidebar until the user chooses; D018, an error, under `navigation: topbar`);
  `composition.filters` (`plate`, the default, or `row`: the filters of a list, a board and a
  calendar on the page, without their plate); `composition.list_tools` (`list`, the default, or
  `header`: the search, the view and the actions of the list of a screen beside its title, a
  related section of a card keeping its own; `init` names `header` for a new `workspace`, `list`
  for a `portal`).
  `THEME` has them (`shell.sidebar`, `composition.filters`, `composition.list_tools`; the
  props `toolbar` of `ResourceList` and `plate` of `FilterRow` are read with them). The tokens of
  the widths of the shell: `size.sidebar` (15rem), `size.sidebar-rail` (4.25rem) and
  `size.content` (80rem, the widest content of a screen, under the top bar too), on which the
  grid of the sidebar and `Screen` are drawn. The guide has a section «Customizing the look»: what a product changes by
  which key or token, and what stays code.
- **`color.selected`**: the color of what is selected or active (the selected row, an item of a
  calendar, a pressed toggle, today, the sorted column: the soft pair `primary-soft`,
  `primary-ink`), the primary by default. `frost` selects in its cedar in both modes and keeps its
  dark honey primary for the actions: one rule, honey the action, cedar the selected.
- **The caption of a screen**: `caption` of `Screen`, a fact beside the title
  (`data-slot="screen-caption"`); `tools` and `caption` of `useScreenPage` are the places of the
  header that a part of the screen fills.

### With `surface: elevated` (a product of 0.12)

What differs from 0.12 besides the table above, by the defaults of the tokens:

- a card (`Card` of shadcn/ui) has `shadow-xs` (was `shadow-sm`), as the other blocks;
- the blocks that were flat have `shadow-xs`; an outline button has `shadow-sm` under the
  pointer;
- a dialog, a toast and a submenu (`DropdownMenuSubContent`) have `shadow-md` (were
  `shadow-lg`);
- the current tab has `shadow-xs` (was `shadow-sm`) and no border in the dark mode (was the
  input);
- the line between the parts of the summary of a card is `--line-10` (was the border).

The colors, the borders and the radii at rest of a card, the summary of a card and an outline
button are those of 0.12 in the light mode, and so are their shadows, except that of a `Card`
of shadcn/ui: `shadow-xs` like the blocks of 0.12 with `shadow-xs` (a tile, a chart, the
summary), where it had `shadow-sm` (above). `assets/playwright/test/shell.spec.ts` compares
them with the classes of a block and of an outline button of 0.12. The focus ring is not that
of 0.12 (3 px of the ring at 50 %, hardly seen on a filled button): a gap of the page and a
solid ring (above). On the copy of a product of 0.12 with `surface: elevated` (the
acceptance below) the screenshots in the light mode differ from those of 0.12 only as the table
above says.

### The keyboard, the focus and the errors

- **A card beside its list** (from `xl`): the list and the card scroll as one page, the line
  between them as high as the window; the rows wrap instead of widening the table; Escape and
  the close button at the end of the header (`action:close`) close the card back to the list
  (Escape not while the focus is in a field, a search or a list of choices, nor when a dialog
  or a menu took it); a
  row selected replaces the entry of the history (Back leaves the list): the navigation of the list in
  `ListCardLayout`, the code of the screens stays that of 0.12. The focus goes to the
  heading of the card that opens and back to its row once it closes; the card is under the `h1`
  of the list (its title an `h2`). Below `lg` the sidebar is a rail until the user expands it.
- **The dialogs** open on their first field and give the focus back to what opened them. A
  form that the user changed asks before it closes (Escape, «Close»: «Keep editing» or «Don't
  save», `state:discard`, `action:discard`), the change read at the moment it closes; a click
  beside it does not close it; the question is gone once the form closes or opens again.
- **The values are checked before they are sent** (`valueErrors` of `@/bazis/react`): in a
  form, the form of an action and the dialog of a transit an empty required field and the end
  of a range of dates before its start (`<x>_to` of `<x>_from`, `<x>_end` of `<x>_start`) are
  marked at their fields, the first focused, nothing sent; the backend validates the rest.
- **The page**: a first link past the navigation to the content (`shell.skip`); the title of the
  page is that of its screen before the name of the product; a path of no screen says not found
  in the layout (the template, `src/app/router.tsx`) instead of going home.
- **A list**: a row opens by Space too and is named by its item; a single page shows its range
  without the buttons of the pages; the reset of the filters keeps them unfolded on a phone and
  gives the focus to the search or the first filter; a picker that is searched waits for the
  items of the text typed (the arrows, Enter selects the first of them once they come).
- **The errors**: the client rejects a request that gets no response with `OfflineError`, which
  the screens say in the language of the interface (`state.offline`, `failureText` of
  `@/bazis/ui/state-panel`), never the text of the browser; a query that the server or the
  network failed is retried once (`createQueryClient` of the template; was three times, the
  error came after seconds); an inline error has its retry too (the filters of a list read their
  fields again), and a retry keeps the focus in the box of its panel.
- **Less motion**: when the system asks for it (`prefers-reduced-motion`), the animations and
  the transitions of the theme end at once.
- **The empty states** (`EmptyState`, `StatePanel`, an empty column of a board) have no dashed
  border: it marks a drop zone of files only.
- **The plate of the filters** is one for a list, a board and a calendar (`FilterRow`), drawn
  as `composition.filters` of the theme says; the sidebar and a screen are as wide as the tokens
  `size.*` (the values of before).

## How to tune the new look

The components read tokens, so a product changes their look in `spec/design/tokens.json` (or
by the layers of `theme.yaml`), never in the components; `bazis_front design` writes them and
D009 checks the contrast of the colors. A product of 0.12 takes the new look by
`palette: frost`, `fonts: golos` and `surface: lifted` in `theme.yaml` (each alone works too),
then `bazis_front design`.
The keys that move the parts of the look (the sidebar, the filters, the tools of a list) and the
tokens below are in one table in «Customizing the look» of the guide (`bazis/contrib/front/AGENTS.md`).

- **The line of the fields**: `color.field-line` at rest, `color.field-line-hover` under the
  pointer, `color.field-line-focus` and the shadow `shadow.field-focus` when focused (the
  defaults: the muted text at 60 % and at 85 %, the ring and the ring of 3 px at 50 %;
  `surface: lifted` keeps the lines and focuses in the primary with a line of 1 px of it).
- **The controls** (the outline, secondary and destructive buttons, the toggles):
  `color.control` and `color.control-hover` (their background), `color.border-control` (their
  border, `transparent` for no contour), the shadows `shadow.lift-05`, `shadow.lift-05-hover`
  and `shadow.lift-05-active`. The current tab has `color.control` and `shadow.lift-05` with a
  transparent border.
- **The blocks** (the cards, the tiles, the charts, the tables, the dialogs): the border
  `color.border-surface`, the shadows `shadow.lift-1` (at rest) and `shadow.lift-2` (under the
  pointer, the dialogs, the menus); `color.surface-light` gives them and the controls a
  gradient from it (none while no layer defines it); `color.shade` is the color of the shadows
  of `surface: lifted`.
- **The primary button**: `shadow.lift-primary`, `shadow.lift-primary-hover`,
  `shadow.lift-primary-active` (none by default).
- **The focus ring**: `shadow.ring-focus` (a gap of the page of 2 px and a solid ring of 2 px
  by default; `surface: lifted`: with a halo of 3 px at 18 % around them).
- **The sidebar and the page**: the gradient of the sidebar `color.sidebar-to`, its light
  `color.sidebar-glow`; the light of the page `color.glow-a` and `color.glow-b` (none while no
  layer defines them; `palette: frost` does); the current link `color.nav-active`,
  `color.nav-active-bar`, `shadow.nav-active`. A color that should follow the sidebar is an
  expression of a role variable (`var(--foreground)`, `var(--primary)`): inside the sidebar it
  is computed on its colors, a literal stays the same everywhere.
- **The selected and the active**: `color.selected` (above); the menus and the popovers:
  `color.popover` (the card by default, a step lighter in the dark mode of `surface: lifted`).
- **A sum and its unit**: a field with `display.currency` shows its sign (`2250 ₽`), so its
  label need not repeat the unit (`Сумма`, not `Сумма, руб.`): a choice of the product, which
  the generator leaves as the backend names the field.
- **The status badge**: `StatusLabel` is `Badge` with `variant="soft"`; a product that wants
  the rounded badge of a sentence back composes its own label with another variant of `Badge`
  in the soft tone of the status (`SOFT_TONES`, `statusTone` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`).
- **The tone `primary` of the statuses**: `color.tone-primary`, the primary by default, with its own soft pair
  (`bg-tone-primary-soft text-tone-primary-ink`: the status badges, the rows and the cards of the tone);
  the soft primary of the interface stays that of `--primary`. A
  palette whose primary is near another tone in a mode gives it a hue of its own: the cedar of
  `frost` in both modes (its dark primary is the honey of the warning), a teal in `forest`, a
  violet in `slate`, a lighter green in the dark `pine`. The tones of every starter palette are
  at least 15 degrees of hue apart in both modes (`TONE_HUES`).

## Upgrading

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # the components, the template, the schemas of the specs
python manage.py bazis_front contract    # contract.json names bazis-front 0.13.0, nothing else changes
python manage.py bazis_front design      # theme.css gains the new variables and utilities (front.W005 until then)
python manage.py bazis_front check
python manage.py bazis_front screens --check   # up to date: the generated code changes only with the new keys
cd frontend && npm install && npx tsc --noEmit && npm run lint && npm test
python manage.py bazis_front shots
```

1. `update` brings the components with their new classes: a copy that the product did not
   change is replaced; a copy it changed (`front.W007`) is merged, with a conflict where it
   changed the same lines as 0.13 (a button of its own in `button.tsx`, the head of its tables
   in `resource-list.tsx` are the likely ones). `update` then writes the other files, ends with
   `CommandError` naming the files in conflict and the file of bazis-front of each
   (`.bazis/base/<asset>@0.13.0/…`), and `update --check` refuses until the markers are
   resolved. Take the lines of bazis-front and apply the change of the product to them again: a
   button of your own is `cn(raised, …)` in the variants of `button.tsx` now, the head of a
   table is on the card by design; then `tsc`, `lint` and `npm test`.
2. The generated screens, the end-to-end tests, `shots.ts`, `contract.ts`, `schema.d.ts` and
   `fonts.ts` stay as they are until the specs use the new keys; `theme.ts` gains `shell` and the
   keys `filters` and `list_tools` of `composition` with their defaults, and `theme.css` only
   gains declarations (the tokens `size.*` among them). A list with `header.search` keeps its
   place; a list without it stays over the list (`list_tools: list`) until `theme.yaml` says
   otherwise.
3. The new look stays opt-in for a product of 0.12: `palette: frost`, `fonts: golos`,
   `surface: lifted` in `theme.yaml` (above).
4. `app-shell` requires `tooltip` (the labels of a rail), which `update` adds with it.
   `ResetFilters` has `shown` (true by default: a hidden reset keeps its place), `FilterRow`
   `line`; a product that composes its own filters may pass them.
5. Texts that the end-to-end tests of a product may read: the titles of the states have no full
   stop (`Nothing here yet`, `Здесь пока ничего нет`), an empty choice of a form is `Choose…`
   (`Выберите…`; the helpers clear a choice by its option `data-value=""`, not its label), the
   description of a transit without one is `Fill in what this step needs.`, and the statuses of
   a filter come in the order of their life, not of their ids.

## Known issues

- **Safari and Firefox were not checked.** The acceptance ran in Chromium (Playwright) only:
  Firefox and WebKit were not installed in its environment. The components use `:has()` (the
  tables, the buttons, the dashboard), `color-mix()`, the variables of Tailwind CSS 4 and a
  portal into the header of a screen (`screen-tools`), all in the versions that Tailwind CSS 4
  supports (Safari 16.4, Firefox 128); the rail and the digits of one width (`tabular-nums`) are
  plain CSS. Report what differs there.
- The filters of a list stay over it in one row that wraps: with many filters at 1280 px they
  take a second row (no preset of folded filters in 0.13).
- The number beside the title of a list is the number alone (`44`, not `44 bookings`): the
  specs have no plural of an entity.
- The initials of a name of one word are one letter.
- The error of a login with wrong credentials is a text of bazis-users (`Учетные данные
  недействительны`, without `ё`), not of bazis-front.
