# bazis-front 0.13.0

Draft: the changes of the components and the focus of a dashboard (packages B and C of 0.13);
the release completes it.

## Added

- **The focus of a dashboard**: `layout: focus` with `hero: {chart, side}` in a dashboard of the
  specs. The chart `hero.chart` (by a date) is large beside the tiles, which are the rows of one
  surface (a rail); the other charts are a column beside the last items under them, one column
  on a narrow screen. The chart shows the value of its current period (the last) large, its
  change on the period before and the figures of `side` beside it: `prev` the period before,
  `peak` the largest value, `total` the sum of the periods, from the values of the chart itself
  (no other query). In the code: `focus` of `Dashboard`, `hero` of `TimeChart` (the list of the
  figures) and of `Chart` (`{bucket, side}`), `layout="rail"` of `StatCard`; the texts
  `chart.hero.*` and `chart.side.*` of the dictionaries. S067 reports a focus without its chart
  or a chart in focus without the focus, a chart in focus that is not of the dashboard or is by
  the status, and the `total` of an average.
- **`kind: distribution`** of a chart by status: the sum of the values large, one stacked bar of
  their shares and their list with the value and the share of each (`value:<key>` with its
  number in `data-value`, as the legend of a ring), drawn without recharts. S068 reports a
  distribution by a date or of an average.

## Changed

The components take the tokens of the surfaces of 0.13 instead of listing their classes: a
block is `surface`, a lifted control `control`, the focus ring `ring-focus` of the theme. A
design without the new layers keeps the values of its tokens (the `:root` of 0.12 only gains
variables), and the look of the components changes as below for every product, whatever its
`surface`; `surface: lifted` (with `palette: frost`) lifts the same components without a
contour.

- **The fields are their bottom line** (an input, a select, a picker of a relationship, a text
  area, the input of a date): was a box with a border and the shadow `shadow-xs`, now a line
  in `color.field-line` (`-hover`, `-focus`, the shadow `shadow.field-focus` when focused),
  with no background; a disabled field is a dashed line (`disabled:border-dashed`, was the
  box at 50 % only). A filter and the range of two dates are one line under the label and
  their controls, the dates separated by a muted dash. A field of your own:
  `fieldClassName` of `@/components/ui/input`; a label with its controls
  `data-slot="field-group"` and `fieldGroupClassName`. The cross that clears the search is in
  the muted color of the text instead of that of the browser.
- **The buttons**: `outline`, `secondary` and `destructive` are a lifted control (`control`:
  `color.control`, `color.border-control`, the shadow `shadow.lift-05`, under the pointer
  `color.control-hover` and `shadow.lift-05-hover`). `secondary` was a muted fill: it is the
  outline button now. `destructive` was a red block: it is a control with red text, never a red
  block beside the main action. The primary button is a flat fill (under the pointer 6 %
  lighter, pressed 7 % darker, instead of 90 % opaque) with `shadow.lift-primary` (none
  without a layer). A disabled button has no shadow (`disabled:shadow-none`).
- **The toggles and the segments**: a toggle (`aria-pressed`: the chips of a filter of yes or
  no, the month and the week of a calendar) is a control, pressed in the soft tone of the
  primary (`bg-primary-soft text-primary-ink`, semibold); the chips were round with a primary
  border when pressed, the segments of the calendar were in a shared frame. The current tab of
  the default `TabsList` is on `color.control` with `shadow.lift-05` and a transparent border
  (was the background with `shadow-sm`).
- **The table reads in ink**: the header was on the muted background in the muted color of the
  text; it is on the card with a line under it (`--line-16`), its text the foreground at 75 %,
  semibold, the sorted column in `text-primary-ink`, the arrows of the sort shown under the
  pointer or the focus only (were at 40 % always). The cells after the first are the
  foreground at 80 % (were the muted color), the lines between the rows `--line-6`, a row under
  the pointer darker (the muted color at 80 % over the card). D009 checks the foreground at 75 %
  and 80 % on the card.
- **The status badge** is in small capitals: 11 px, semibold, uppercase, on the soft background
  of its tone (`variant="soft"` of `Badge`; was a rounded badge of a sentence).
- **The charts**: the scale shows 0 and the largest value only, the grid its zero line; the bars
  of a `TimeChart` before the current period (the last) are lighter (55 %), as those of a
  `Chart` with the new prop `periods` (values of your own stay alike); the title of a chart is
  in small capitals (`font-eyebrow`).
- **The blocks that were flat are lifted** by `shadow.lift-1` like the cards (`surface`): the
  result of an action and of a background task, the progress of a task, the grid and the list
  of the day of a calendar, its events (`shadow.lift-05`), the hero of a dashboard, a map and
  the thumbnails of the files. The summary of a card is one surface with a line between its
  parts (was the border color between cells 1 px apart).
- **The dialogs** are a surface on the card (were on the background), rounder (`rounded-2xl`),
  with `shadow.lift-2`; the menus, the popovers, the selects and the toasts have the border of
  the surfaces and `shadow.lift-2`.
- **One focus ring**: `ring-focus` of the theme (`shadow.ring-focus` over the shadow of the
  element, which stays) on every component: the buttons, the tabs, the badges, the checkboxes,
  the switches, the calendars, the links of the shell, the markers of a map, the close buttons
  of a dialog, a sheet and a toast (on the focus of the keyboard only, without the offset ring
  of shadcn/ui). A control in error keeps its red border, no red ring. A row of a table keeps
  its ring inside it (3 px of the ring at 50 %). What differs from the focus of 0.12:
  - the border of a button, a checkbox, a badge, a select and a tab is no longer in the color of
    the ring when focused (`focus-visible:border-ring` is gone): the ring alone shows the focus;
  - the solid rings of 0.12 are the ring of 3 px at 50 % of the theme: `ring-2 ring-ring` of the
    close button of a toast and of the thumbnail of a file (its ring under the pointer stays),
    `ring-[3px] ring-ring` of a marker of a map, `focus:ring-2` with its offset of the close
    button of a dialog and of a sheet;
  - a tab has no `focus-visible:outline-1` any more.
- **The tabs**: the track of the default `TabsList` is the muted background at 80 % with an
  inner shadow (was the muted background).
- **Under the pointer** a tile and a quick link of a dashboard and a card of a list that opens
  are lifted by `shadow.lift-2` without the border `border-primary/40` (was both, with
  `shadow-md`).
- **The tiles of a dashboard on a phone**: only the last of an odd number of tiles takes the
  whole row (was every tile with a value), so the row of a phone has no hole.
- **The last items of a dashboard** (`RecentList`): the title is in small capitals
  (`font-eyebrow`, was 14 px medium), a row is at least 56 px high with a line `--line-6`
  between the rows (was 44 px under a coarse pointer only, without a line).
- **The subtitle of a screen** is 12 px under its sticky header (was right under its line);
  the panel of the card beside a list leaves room for the shadows of its blocks at
  its right and at its end.
- **Inside the sidebar** the surfaces and the controls are computed on the colors of the
  sidebar (`--background` and `--card` are `--sidebar`): a card or an outline button that a
  product puts in `sidebar-tools` was white on a dark sidebar, it is on the sidebar now with its
  text. D009 checks the text of the sidebar on a control at rest and under the pointer and the
  focused line of a field on the sidebar (3:1). The avatars (`bg-avatar-<n>`) are colors of the
  content: not for the sidebar.
- **The defaults of the controls in the dark mode** are those of shadcn/ui:
  `color.control` the input at 30 % (translucent), `color.control-hover` at 50 %,
  `color.border-control` the input (the border of a card in the light mode). D009 reads a
  translucent background over the page.

### With `surface: elevated` (a product of 0.12)

What differs from 0.12 besides the lines above, by the defaults of the tokens:

- a card (`Card` of shadcn/ui) has `shadow-xs` (was `shadow-sm`), as the other blocks;
- the blocks that were flat have `shadow-xs`; an outline button has `shadow-sm` under the
  pointer;
- a dialog, a toast and a submenu (`DropdownMenuSubContent`) have `shadow-md` (were
  `shadow-lg`);
- the current tab has `shadow-xs` (was `shadow-sm`) and no border in the dark mode (was the
  input);
- the line between the parts of the summary of a card is `--line-10` (was the border).

The colors, the borders, the radii and the shadows at rest of a card, the summary of a card and
an outline button are those of 0.12 in the light mode (`assets/playwright/test/shell.spec.ts`
compares them with the classes of 0.12). The focus ring of `shadow.ring-focus` is that of most
components of 0.12 (3 px of the ring at 50 %), with the differences of the focus above.
