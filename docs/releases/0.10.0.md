# bazis-front 0.10.0

The passes of the scenarios that the acceptance of 0.9 on two products asked for: a photo to
upload, a move with a payload, the absence of a transit, the hints of the keys that are not
steps, a socket that connects again, and the choices of a payload without their labels. It
requires `bazis>=2.17.0`, as 0.9 did; the format of the contract stays 11.

## A1: the scenarios

### Added

- **`e2e/fixtures/photo.png` in the template.** A small image (64×48 PNG, 4 KB) that a scenario
  uploads into an image field, `upload: {field: photo, file: photo.png}`, without a fixture of
  its own. It is the first binary file of the template: `init` and `update` copy it as it is
  (never stamped nor merged), and prettier leaves it out with `e2e/fixtures/`.
- **`move` with a payload.** `move: {where, to, payload: {...}}` moves an item of a board to a
  status that a transition with a payload leads to, the payload filled in the dialog of the
  transit as `transit` fills it (`move({where, to, payload})` of the helpers). Before, such a
  move was P024 and the transit had to run on the card. The fields of the payload are checked
  as those of `transit` (P024); a move without the payload that it needs is P024 with the
  payload to give in its hint, and one with a payload to a status that only a transition
  without one leads to is P024 too. A `$<name>` remembered before is taken in a payload of a
  move as in that of a transit.
- **`expect: {transit_absent: <id>}`.** A transit that the card of the item (`transitions:
  true`) does not offer the role: the helper `expectTransitAbsent(id)` waits for the transits
  of the card to load, then expects no `transit:<id>`. P024 for a transit that is not of the
  workflow, P022 off such a card, and P022 when `access` grants the role the transit on every
  item (`all`) while the item is in its source status: the card shows it, so the expectation
  cannot pass.
- **The walk knows the status of the item that a scenario created**: its initial status, then
  the status that its transits and moves lead to, or the `status` that an `expect` on its card
  reads; another item, or another screen, has none known. `transit_absent` reads it.
- **`expect: {action_absent: edit}`** on a card with `edit: true`: the card of a role that may
  not change the item has no edit. The P022 of an absent action that the screen does not have
  names the other expectations of an absence (`transit_absent`, `field_readonly`, a test of the
  refusal of the backend).
- **P037** (warning): a field of the payload of a transition has choices without their labels
  (`enum` without `enumDict`: a `Literal`, or a core before 2.22, which gives the labels of the
  `TextChoices` in the payloads of the transits): the dialog of the transit offers them by their
  values. As S058 for the bodies of the actions.
- **`bazis_front version`** prints the installed bazis-front. `manage.py bazis_front --version`
  is the version of Django, as for any management command.

### Fixed

- **The visual check no longer reports the socket of a page that was left.** Chromium writes
  "WebSocket connection to … failed" to the console when a page is left before the handshake of
  its socket, and the client of the socket connects again by itself; the shot reported it as
  V012. Such a message is now reported only when the socket of the page (`socket:open` of
  `LiveQuery`) is not open within 2 s: a socket that does not connect is still V012.
- **The hint of a step with a parameter of a helper.** `created` (of `openItem`, `select` and
  `move`) and `saved` (of `submit`) are given by the generator, not keys of the steps: P002 of a
  step that has one says what it is and to remove it, instead of the schema to follow. The
  table of the steps of the guide shows the keys of the steps and the helpers without them.
- **A conflict of `package.json` offers no file of bazis-front.** The error of `update` with
  conflicts said to take "the file of bazis-front listed for one" also when only `package.json`
  conflicted, which has none; it says so only when a conflict names one.

### Changed

- The guide says which operations `access` has (`view`, `add`, `change`, `delete`, `transit`):
  the other permissions of a role, the status history (`history`) or the `access_action` of a
  custom action (`export`), are declared in `roles.py` alone.

## A2: the card and the forms of the actions

### Added

- **A section of a card shown by the status of its item.** `card.sections[].when: {status:
  [...]}` shows the section only while the item is in one of these statuses of the workflow of
  the entity (`when` of `CardSection`, read from the `status` of the item; a tab shows what it
  has). The edit of the card keeps its fields. **S059** (error): a status that is not of the
  workflow, or a section with `when` on an entity without a workflow. The walk of the scenarios
  reads the known status of the item (that of the expectation, else that of the walk):
  `values` of a field of a section that is not shown then is P022; `field_absent` of it passes.
  The sample shows the report of a task once it is done.
- **The values that the fields of a custom action start with.** `values: {<property>: <value>}`
  of a custom action (its dialog) and of the form of an action (`form.values`) gives its fields
  their values when they open, and again on `action:again`, over the `default` of the body: a
  value of the type of the property, or for a date a day of the browser, `today`, `today+<n>`,
  `today-<n>`, `month_start`, `month_end` (then ` HH:MM` for a date-time), or `YYYY-MM-DD`
  (`values` of `ActionForm` and `useCustomAction`, the type `StartValues`). **S060** (error): a
  value of no field, of another type or out of the choices of its property, a day of the
  browser of a property that is not a date, a time of a date. The export of the sample takes
  the tasks created from a day (`created_from`), the first of the month by default.

## Upgrading

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # the helpers of e2e/bazis/, e2e/fixtures/photo.png of the template, the schemas of the specs
python manage.py bazis_front check       # P022 of an absence that cannot pass, P037, S059, S060
python manage.py bazis_front e2e
```

1. A frontend that has a file of its own at `e2e/fixtures/photo.png` makes `update` refuse
   (`exists and is not the copy of template`) and write nothing: rename the file of the product
   (and its `upload` steps), then `update` again.
2. A scenario that moved an item by `transit` on its card only because the move needed a
   payload may move it on the board, `move: {where, to, payload}`.
3. A step with `created: true` or `saved: true` (P002): remove the key, the generator gives it.
