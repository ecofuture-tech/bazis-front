# bazis-front 0.3.0

The screens of a product are generated from its specs. It requires `bazis>=2.16.0`, as
0.1.0 did. The template renders the generated screens, and a screen written by hand stays
one, declared `custom` in its spec.

0.2.0 was never published: this release includes its changes, the design, the new
components and the visual check, listed in [0.2.0](0.2.0.md).

## What is new

- **The screens of the specs.** `manage.py bazis_front screens` writes
  `src/screens/generated/` from `spec/product.yaml`, `spec/screens/*.yaml` and the icons of
  the design: a component per screen (`list`, `card`, `form`, `dashboard`, `board`,
  `calendar`, with their actions, sections, tabs, related lists, bulk actions, highlights,
  filters and map), the routes, the navigation, the entities, the providers and the tools
  that the packages and the fields need, the texts and the name of the product. `--check`
  and the system check `front.W010` compare them with the specs byte for byte, without
  Node; an edited generated file is refused unless `--force`.
- **The texts in the languages of the product.** A title, a description, a label of the
  specs, and the `title` of an entity, is a map of the languages of `languages`
  (`{ru: …, en: …}`) or a key of the dictionaries; the generated texts are
  `GENERATED_MESSAGES`, merged with the dictionaries of the frontend.
- **The navigation** of `product.yaml`: the links to the screens with their `label` (the
  title of the screen by default), `icon` and `requires`, and the groups of links with their
  title.
- **What the specs do not say.** `custom` (`true`, or `{module, export}` for a module with
  several screens) keeps a screen written by hand with its generated route, link and texts;
  `extend` adds the slots and the props of `src/screens/<id>/extend.tsx` to a generated
  screen.
- **The contract** (format 6) gives a resource `create`, the fields that its create route
  takes, which the generated forms and the add of a calendar use, and `custom_actions`, the
  custom actions of its route set (their name in the path, whether of an item, the method,
  `access_action` and the JSON Schema of their body).
- **The custom actions in the specs.** `primitive: custom` calls a custom action of the route
  set (a POST of `http_post`): a button of a card, of each row of a list
  (`list.row_actions`), of the toolbar of a list for an action of the route set, or a bulk
  action (`list.bulk` with `action`), with the dialog of the properties of its body that it
  asks for (`fields`) or its confirmation (`confirm`), and its toast (`message`); shown as
  the backend allows it, once per item at a time. An action of an item and one of the route
  set may share a name (`/{item_id}/export/` and `/export/`). `check` reports S032 for one
  that the contract does not have, of the wrong kind (an item or the route set), with fields
  that its body does not take, or on the rows or in a bulk action one that requires of the
  item another access than its view, change or delete (a list tells of each row only these);
  a scenario runs one of a card (`action`, `fill`, `submit`).
- **The components.** A `MapProvider` without `tiles` keeps the tiles of the one above it: a
  product sets its tiles once, around `GeneratedProviders` in `src/app/router.tsx`.
- **A card without tabs** shows its related lists and its history under its sections
  (`after` of `ResourceCard`, merged with that of an extension like `children`), its
  transits under its heading.
- **A related list in a card next to its list** (`split` from `xl`) shows the cards of a phone
  instead of a table scrolled sideways (`useBesideList`); a grid of cards has the columns of
  its own width, not of the page.
- **The toolbar of a list** keeps its actions on the row of its search and filters while they
  fit: the search narrows down to 10rem first.
- **A chart by status** draws a status in the color of its tone, that of its badge and of its
  tiles (`statuses` of the theme), no longer in a color of the series.
- **A deleted item is not read again** (`useDestroy`): a screen still showing it (a card
  next to the list of a bulk deletion) shows it not found at once; a card left by the
  navigation of its deletion (`useDestroyCardAction` with `then`, `{leaving: true}`) keeps
  its data until then; its queries are removed once nothing shows them.
- **The options of the commands by their full names**: `bazis_front screens --forc` is an
  error, no longer `--force`.
- **The screenshots of each role**: a screen is also shot as each other of its roles, in the
  first color mode only (`<screen> as <role>`); a calendar with `add` is also shot with its add form open.
- **Whether the user may add** (`useCan(path, 'add')`, the actions and the links with
  `can: 'add'`) is `for_create` of a list of one item: a user who may only view asks for no
  runtime schema of the create the backend refuses (the schema without that meta).
- **The drag and drop of a board** is told to the screen readers in the language of the
  interface, by the names of the item and of the status, never an id.
- **A long value of an item never widens the page**: the grids of the cards, of the board,
  of the calendar and of the dashboard bound their column, and their texts wrap.
- **The screenshots** check the page at the width of a desktop too (V014), and once the
  texts of the items are long (V015: a text of 120 characters put in their cells and fields,
  `data-text` of `FieldValue`, not in a date, a number or the label of a choice, for the
  check, then taken out). V002 measures the layout viewport of the phone:
  the window of a mobile browser grows to a wider page, so that it found none before. V016
  warns of a list, a board, a calendar or the last items of a dashboard shot without an item.
- **The dates of the end-to-end tests** are relative to the day of the run: the guide asks
  `e2e_data` for them (a calendar opens on the month of today), and `expect: {day: …}` takes
  `today`, `today+<n>` and `today-<n>`, and so does `fill` of a date or a date-time
  (`today 10:30`).
- **The confirmation of a deletion** is titled by `title` of the action, a question
  (`Delete this item?` by default), no longer by the label of its button.
- **The add of a calendar** opens its form starting with the selected day, which the user
  may change (`initial` of `ResourceForm` and `useResourceForm`), no longer fixed read-only:
  a date-time field starts at the beginning of the day in the time zone of the browser and
  the user sets its time (it was the day alone, read as midnight in UTC, which the backend
  compared with aware dates).
- **The items of a calendar** have the time of a date-time, and each field of their label
  on a line of the width of the day, instead of the fields sharing one line; a week at the
  width of a phone lists its days without the height of a column.
- **A text of a table wraps** in its column (the label of a related item, a string), never a
  date or a number: a long value makes the row higher, not the table wider.
- **An error of a form on a field that it does not show** is named by the title of the
  field, not its name.
- **`bazis_front shots`** starts its dev server on a free port without `E2E_PORT`, no longer
  on 5173, which the dev server of the developer has; a 404 of the fields of the filters
  (`route_filter_fields/`) is reported (V013), and a 500 of the proxy of the dev server that
  did not reach the backend says so.
- **New codes.** The specs: S027 (a text not in every language of the product), S028 and
  S029 (the code of a custom screen, a screen with two implementations), S030 (the
  navigation), S031 (the extension of a screen), S033 (two screens or two actions that the
  generated code would name alike), S034 and S035 (the add of a calendar, the create forms of
  a screen), S036 and S037 (the keys of the texts and of the dictionaries, warnings), S038 (a
  screen of an item without `:id`, but a custom one), S032 (a custom action), S039 (a field twice in a sort), S040 (the
  filters of a route set whose `actions` leave out the fields of its filters; a report that the navigation does not link is not checked), S041 (warning: the add of a calendar by a date-time field that the create route takes, without it among its `fields`; else S034), P032 (two entities that the generated
  code would name alike, an error that may stop `check` and `screens` of a product of 0.1.0),
  D013 (an icon that lucide-react does not have); the screenshots: V014, V015, V016, V017 (a
  text cut to a few characters) and V018 (a table that grows with long texts). The system check `front.W010` (the
  generated screens stale, edited or missing).

## Migrating from 0.1.0

0.2.0 was never published (see [0.2.0](0.2.0.md)): a product of 0.1.0 goes to 0.3.0 in one
run, with the steps of both.

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # exit 1 with conflicts is expected; resolve those it lists (steps 2, 3)
(cd frontend && npm install)             # before the contract; `tsc` stays red until `screens` (step 8)
python manage.py bazis_front contract    # contract.json of format 6, schema.d.ts
python manage.py bazis_front design      # theme.css and the new fonts.css
# steps 5 and 6: `custom` in the specs of the screens written by hand, `navigation` in product.yaml
python manage.py bazis_front check
python manage.py bazis_front screens     # also copies the components that they import
(cd frontend && npm install)             # their npm dependencies, which `screens` adds
python manage.py bazis_front e2e
python manage.py bazis_front shots --no-run
cd frontend && npx tsc --noEmit && npm run lint && npm test && npm run build
```

1. **`update`** copies the assets that the new version requires and the frontend lacks (`dashboard`,
   `stat-card`, the shadcn/ui components on Radix, the libraries of charts, dates and drag
   and drop), merges the template and the copies, and lists the files that conflict. It then
   ends with exit 1: the files are updated, the lock and `.bazis/base/` are those of the new
   version, and a second `update` does not merge again. Run `npm install` at once (the
   `@fontsource` packages, the new libraries), before `contract`, which generates
   `schema.d.ts` with the frontend. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm test` fail with `TS2307`
   (`@/screens/generated/*`) until `screens` (step 8): `update` says "run screens".
2. **`src/app/router.tsx`** that the product changed conflicts: replace the whole file by that
   of the template, `frontend/.bazis/base/template@<version>/src/app/router.tsx`. Taking the
   side of bazis-front in each conflict is not enough: the lines of the product merged
   without a conflict, such as its imports, stay. Then apply the other changes of the
   product to it again: its own providers, the routes that are not screens of the specs,
   the tiles of its maps (a `MapProvider tiles` around `GeneratedProviders`). Move its links
   to `navigation` (step 6).
3. **The rest of the template.** `src/i18n/en.ts` and `src/i18n/ru.ts` that the product changed
   conflict too, the template having removed the texts of its home: keep the side of the
   frontend while its screens use them, and remove the keys that no screen reads once they
   are generated (the texts of a generated screen are those of its spec, in
   `GENERATED_MESSAGES`). `src/app/product.ts` and the home `src/screens/home/index.tsx`
   are no longer in the template: `update` keeps them and says what replaces them. The name
   of the product is `product.name` of `product.yaml` (it replaces `PRODUCT_NAME`). If the
   product put `MAP_TILES` in `product.ts` (the guide of 0.1.0 suggested it), move it to
   `router.tsx` (or another file) before you delete `product.ts`: the `MapProvider` of the
   router imports it. Delete the home, or keep it with `custom: true` in
   `spec/screens/home.yaml`.
4. **The theme and the contract.** `design` after `update`: `src/index.css` imports
   `src/bazis/generated/fonts.css`, which only `design` writes, and the theme of 0.1.0 lacks
   `list_phone`. To take a palette, fonts or a shape of `theme.yaml`, remove the colors and
   the fonts (and the radius) from `tokens.json`: that of 0.1.0 defines them all, which
   hide the layers (D012); keep there only what the product changes, such as its brand.
   `contract.json` is of format 6: `check` reports `C002` until it is exported again, then
   warns of the fields without a title (S018), the entities shown by their id (S019) and
   the entities without an icon (D011).
5. **`custom` on every screen written by hand**, before `check` and `screens`. Every screen of
   a product of 0.1.0 is written by hand: give each spec `custom` (`true` for
   `src/screens/<id>/index.tsx`, `{module, export}` otherwise). Without it `screens` refuses
   (S029: a screen with two implementations) and writes nothing, as `check` does once the
   router is that of the template (step 2). Then `e2e` and `shots`
   behave as before. Later, screen by screen, remove `custom` and the file, move its texts
   to the spec and run `screens` and `shots` again; what the specs do not say goes to
   `extend`.
6. **`navigation`.** Move the links of the router to `navigation` of `product.yaml` with
   their `icon` and `requires`. The `label` of a link is the title of the screen by
   default, which is a text in one language: a link whose old label was a key of the
   dictionaries of the product (`t('nav.tasks')`) keeps it as `label: nav.tasks` (the
   dictionaries `src/i18n/*.ts` stay), or becomes `{en: …, ru: …}`; so does the `title` of
   a screen (S027 warns of the texts that miss a language of the product).
7. **`check`** has no errors (S029 is told for every screen written by hand that lacks
   `custom`, as soon as the router of the template imports the generated screens, step 2). It also reports the new codes (below).
8. **`screens`** renders `src/screens/generated/` and copies the components that the generated
   screens import and the frontend lacks (`calendar` and `date-picker` for the dates of
   the forms), setting their npm dependencies (`react-day-picker`): run `npm install` again,
   else `tsc` fails (`TS2307`). From here `npx tsc --noEmit` is green again.
9. **`e2e`, `shots --no-run`, then `tsc`, lint, tests and the build.** `tsc` finds the code of
   the product that uses what changed (the list below).

The API of the copies that 0.2.0 changed (never published; `npx tsc --noEmit` finds the code
of the product that uses it):

- `lazyScreen` shows the loading state of the screens in its own `Suspense`, and the router
  of the template has none: a `src/app/router.tsx` that the product changed may conflict on
  its imports; remove the imports of `Suspense` and `StatePanel` that nothing uses any more.
- The filters of a list are the select of Radix, a boolean filter its chips: a test of the
  product that chose a filter with `selectOption` chooses it with `fill` of the helpers, as
  the generated tests do. The panels of the tabs of a card (`card.tabs`) stay in the DOM,
  hidden: a test opens the tab before it fills or reads a control of it.

The API of the copies that 0.3.0 changed:

- `payloadErrors` of `@/bazis/ui/transit-bar` takes `message`, a function of an error to
  its text (`useErrorMessage()` of `@/bazis/react`), in place of `t`.
- `BoardDrag` of `@/bazis/ui/resource-board` (a drag and drop of the product in place of
  `board-dnd`): its `Column` takes `label` (the name of its status) and its `Card` takes
  `label` (the name of its item, null without one), told to the screen readers.
- `useCan(path, 'add')` (the create actions, the links with `can: 'add'`) reads
  `GET <path>?page[limit]=1&meta=for_create` instead of `<path>schema_create/` (which it
  still reads without that meta): a mock of the backend in a test of the product answers
  that list.
- `useDestroy(path)` shows a deleted item still shown as not found (an error 404 without a
  request) instead of reading it again; `useDestroy(path, {leaving: true})` keeps its data
  while its screen is left.
- The helpers of the end-to-end tests: `App.confirm(id)` confirms the dialog of an action
  (`action:confirm-<id>`), which a deletion of a card shows: a test of the product that
  deletes by `action(id)` confirms it then; `expectDay` takes `today±N`.
- `formatNumber` and `formatDecimal` of `@/bazis/i18n` (and the numbers of the texts)
  group the digits by thousands from five digits only: `1234` (it was `1,234`), `12,345`;
  `{useGrouping: true}` groups them as before. A test of the product that reads a number
  of four digits reads it without the separator.
- The validation errors of the forms and of the actions (`errors` of `useResourceForm`,
  `ActionMutation`) are in the language of the interface (`useErrorMessage`), no longer
  the text of the backend: a test of the product that reads one reads the translated
  text.
- `ResourceCard` takes `after`, rendered under its sections and its tabs: the related
  lists and the history of a card without tabs go there (`children` stays under the
  heading, for the transits).
- The deletion of a card (`useDestroyCardAction` of `@/bazis/ui/screens`, new) asks
  `card.delete_title` (a question) and says `card.delete_confirm` (what the deletion
  does).
- `add.onAdd` of `ResourceCalendar` gets the value of its field on the selected day: the
  day of a date as before, the start of the day in the time zone of the browser (ISO) of
  a date-time instead of the day; give it to the form as `initial` (the user may change
  it), not `fixed`.
- `permission` of a `ListAction` and of a `CardAction` also takes the name of a custom
  action (`action_<name>`), allowed by `custom_actions` of the meta of the list or of the
  item.
- New: `@/bazis/ui/screens` (the actions and the extensions of the generated screens,
  `useCustomAction`), `customActionAt`, `useAnyAction` and `useAnyItemAction` of
  `@/bazis/ui/resource` (a custom action by its name in the contract and whether it is of
  an item),
  `useErrorMessage` and the type `DestroyPath` of `@/bazis/react`, `errorText` of the
  client, `useBesideList`, `NavEntry` and `NavGroup` of `@/bazis/ui/app-shell` (`items`
  of `AppShell` takes groups of links; `useNavItems` still gives the links), `initial` of
  `useResourceForm` and `ResourceForm` (the values a create starts with, which the user
  may change), `fieldTitle` of `@/bazis/ui/resource`.

The guide for the agents (`bazis/contrib/front/AGENTS.md`, "Migrating the code of the
product") lists the same steps.
