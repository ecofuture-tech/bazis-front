# bazis-front 0.6.1

A patch of 0.6.0: the contract with bazis-statusy 2.15, the averages of the tiles rounded to
zero, and the id of the menu of the top bar kept from the screens. It requires `bazis>=2.17.0`,
as 0.6.0 did; the format of the contract stays 9.

## Fixed

- **The history of the transits is not a custom action of the product.** bazis-statusy 2.15
  gives every statusy route set the route `GET /{item_id}/status_history/`
  (`action_status_history`, the kind `other` in `x-bazis`). The contract listed it in
  `custom_actions` of the resource beside the actions of the product; it now leaves it out,
  as it leaves out the transits (`resources.NOT_CUSTOM`), whatever version of bazis-statusy
  is installed: the contract of a product is the same with 2.14 and 2.15 but for its route
  in `actions` and in the OpenAPI. A custom action of the product declared with GET stays in
  `custom_actions`: the code reads it (`useActionQuery`, `useItemActionQuery`), and no button
  or form of the specs runs it (S032).
- **An average rounded to zero has no sign.** `StatCard` rounded a small negative average to
  the decimal places of its field as `-0` (`-0.4` with none, `-0.00` for `-0.004` with two);
  it shows `0` and `0.00`, and a negative average that does not round to zero keeps its
  sign (`-1.26`).

## Added

- **S055** (error): a screen with the id `more`. Its links (`nav:more`) would be marked as the
  menu of the links that the top bar has no room for, which the end-to-end tests and the
  screenshots find by that mark.

## Upgrading

- `manage.py bazis_front update` brings `stat-card`.
- A screen with the id `more` fails `manage.py bazis_front check` (S055): rename its file and
  its id, then the links to it (`open`, `then`, the navigation), and run
  `manage.py bazis_front screens`, `e2e` and `shots`.
- With bazis-statusy 2.15, export the contract again (`manage.py bazis_front contract`): its
  OpenAPI has the route of the history.
