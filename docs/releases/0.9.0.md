# bazis-front 0.9.0

The home of a product stops needing code for what a role may do (a tile by the right to add,
the start of a guest, the number of the answer in the title of a result), and `check` stops
asking for what a role cannot do (P033) or hiding the cause of a broken flow mapping (P002).
A public form takes the photos of a guest, an action answers a file that its form saves, a
scenario follows several users, and a transit asks for an item: the format of the contract
is 11.

## Requirements

- bazis-uploadable 2.8 for the files of a public form (the anonymous uploads and
  `UploadedFileId`); with 2.7 the contract has no anonymous uploads and a public form with
  files is S057.
- bazis 2.21 for the labels of the choices of a body (`enumDict` of the `TextChoices` of a
  model); with an older core the forms offer the choices by their values (S058, a warning).
  bazis-front still installs with bazis 2.17.

## Features

- **`requires: {can: add}` of a tile, a chart and the last items of a dashboard** (as the
  `requires` of a link of the navigation): the part is shown to the users whom the backend
  lets add an item of its entity, not only view them (the tile "New" of a dispatcher, not of a
  master). `Viewable` takes `can`, `Dashboard` takes `requires` in the props of its tiles and
  of its last items. S030 reports `can: add` of an entity without a create route in the contract.
- **A guest who opens `/` is led to the first public link of the navigation** (`GUEST_HOME`
  of `src/screens/generated/routes.tsx`, null when `/` is public itself or no link is): the
  screen of a session at `/` no longer sends him to the login. No key of the specs: the order
  of `navigation` says which link is first.
- **The title of the result of the form of an action shows the answer**
  (`result.title: Order No. {number}`), as the message does; S053 checks the parameters of
  the title too.

## Changes

- **P033 is not reported to a role that cannot add.** A scenario of a role whose `access` has
  no `add` for the entity cannot create the item it changes: it opens the item that
  `e2e_data` creates afresh at every run. The hint of P033 says so for the roles that the
  specs leave undeclared.
- **P002 and S002 tell what a piece of a text is.** A key with a space or a letter beyond
  ASCII in a mapping that the schema refuses is the text after the comma of a flow mapping
  (`{entrance_info: 1 floor, 3 rooms}`): the hint says to quote the text.
- **After the login a user is at home**, not on the public page he signed in from: `signIn`
  of the router of the template opens `/login` without `from`; a screen of a session still
  comes back to itself after the login.
- **The search of bazis 2.20 in the contract.** With bazis 2.20 a route set without
  `search_fields` searches the text fields of its LIST schema: `contract` lists them in
  `search` of its resource, so its pickers search and S012 and S013 are not reported for it;
  `search_fields = []` turns the search off. The older cores are supported as before.

## Format of the contract, files of a public form, file answer, sessions of the scenarios, relationship of a payload

- **Format 11 of the contract.** A custom action has `file`, whether its answer (200) is a
  file, content of another type than JSON (a `response_class` with a `media_type`, such as
  `text/csv`, also `text/html`; `application/json` and the `+json` types, such as
  `application/vnd.api+json` of `JsonApiResponse`, are JSON); the section `uploadable` has
  `anonymous`, whether guests upload files (`anonymous_uploads()` of bazis-uploadable 2.8:
  `BAZIS_FILE_UPLOAD_ANONYMOUS` with a size limit), and `anonymous_types`, the media types
  they may (empty when they may not; false and empty with bazis-uploadable 2.7). `check`
  reports C002 until the contract is exported again.
- **The files of a public form** (bazis-uploadable 2.8): a property of the body of an action
  that takes the ids of uploaded files (`UploadedFileId`, `list[UploadedFileId]`: `x-relation`
  of its JSON Schema) is a file field of its dialog and of its form, `FileField` for one,
  `FilesField` for a list. A guest uploads before the submit, without a session, the types of
  the anonymous uploads (`FileFieldProvider guest`, `ANONYMOUS_ACCEPT`) up to `max_size`; the
  file chosen is shown from the browser (a guest may not read it back); a refusal of the
  backend (413, 422 of the type or the name, 429, 401) is the error of the field. A public
  screen with such a property while the contract has no anonymous uploads is S057 (an error);
  a scenario uploads into it (`upload: {field: photos, file: photo.png}`; `fill` is P022). The
  guide has "Anonymous uploads": the settings, attaching the files in the action
  (`FileUpload.objects.anonymous(tokens)`), `files_visible`, the clean-up.
- **`FilesField`** (file-field), the control of several files: chosen or dropped at once,
  uploaded one after the other, listed with a cancel or a remove until the submit (the local
  copies released), the value the ids in their order; `FilesProvider` takes it as
  `listControl` for the to-many relationships to the files. `useUploads` of
  `@/bazis/ui/resource` tells a form that a file of a field uploads: `ResourceForm`, the form
  and the dialog of an action wait for it; the form of an action is no longer sent twice by a
  second submit while its request runs.
- **The id of an item in a body by its `x-relation`**: a property whose JSON Schema names the
  resource of its ids is the picker of that resource (`relation` of the `AttributeField` of
  `objectFields`), as a property named as a relationship of the entity; not S054.
- **S058** (warning): a property of a body with choices without their labels (a `Literal`, or
  a core before 2.21): the form offers them by their values; type it by the `TextChoices` of
  the model.
- **The form of an action that answers a file saves it** (`primitive: form` with
  `form.action`, no code): an export of a few hundred rows is a route with a `response_class`
  and a `Content-Disposition`, the form downloads the file with the token, saves it, then
  shows its result, whose only parameter is `{filename}`, the name it is saved under (S053).
  Only a form runs such an action: a button of a dialog does not save it (S032). The client
  has `actionFile(path, name, body)` (`{blob, name}`, as `download`), the hooks
  `useActionFile(path, name)` (saves the file, resolves to its name, refetches the resource),
  and `Backend.file` of `@/bazis/ui/testing` takes the method of the request (`POST` for an
  action). A long export stays a task of bazis-bg with `TaskProgress`.
- **A scenario of several users**: the step `as: <role>` runs the next steps in the session
  of the role (a context of the browser of its own, its test user logged in the first time;
  P004, P025), with its own screen and form; `remember: {<name>: <property>}` after the result
  of the form of an action keeps a text or a number of its answer, `$<name>` in `fill`,
  `where`, `values` and `payload` of the next steps (P022). An item found by a remembered value
  is one the run made: changed without P033 (remember what the server made, not the echo of a
  text typed). Two roles whose sessions the tests would name alike (`team-lead`, `team_lead`:
  `asTeamLead`) are P032. The helpers have `sessionAs(app, role)` and
  `answer(property)`, which reads `answer:<property>` that `ActionResult` renders hidden for
  each text and number of the answer.
- **The id of an item in the payload of a transit**: a field of the payload is
  `{id, relation: <entity>}`, the property named as a to-one relationship of the entity to it
  (`master: UUID` of the payload and the relationship `master`; P017 otherwise, P005 for an
  unknown entity). The dialog of the transit, on a card and on a board, asks for it with the
  picker of the relationship: `relatedField(path, property, label?, resource?)` of
  `@/bazis/ui/resource`, which the forms of the actions use too. A scenario fills it by the
  label of the item: `transit: {id: assign, payload: {master: Bob Stone}}`.

## Upgrading

```bash
pip install -U bazis-front
python manage.py bazis_front update      # the components of the dashboard, the screens, the resource and the transit bar, the router of the template, the schemas of the specs, the helpers of e2e/bazis/
python manage.py bazis_front contract    # format 11
python manage.py bazis_front screens
python manage.py bazis_front check
```

1. `update` takes the new `router.tsx` of the template (3 lines: `GUEST_HOME`, the redirect of
   `/` for a guest and `signIn` without `from`); a product that changed the router gets a
   3-way merge: for a conflict at `RequireSession` or `signIn`, take the file of the
   template and apply the changes of the product to it again. `screens` writes
   `GUEST_HOME` into `routes.tsx`.
2. A product with public screens whose `/` is a screen of a session: a guest who opens `/`
   now sees the first public link of the navigation instead of the login page (the sign-in
   stays in the layout); order `navigation` to choose it.
3. A scenario of a role that cannot add no longer has P033: nothing to change. `check` has
   no new errors for a product of 0.8 but C002 until `contract` and S032 for a button of an
   action that answers a file (whose dialog read the file as JSON: run it as the form of the
   action); the title of a result with a `{parameter}` is shown with the answer.
4. A custom screen that saved the file of an action itself (the CSV in a JSON answer, a
   `Blob` saved by hand) may become the form of the action: the action answers the file with
   its `response_class`, the spec shows `{filename}` in its result.
5. A test of `e2e/custom/` of two users (a second `browser.newContext()`) or of a number read
   from the text of a result may become a scenario with `as` and `remember`; the generated
   tests of the scenarios of 0.8 are the same.
6. A payload property named as a to-one relationship of the entity is now asked for with its
   picker instead of a text input of its id, also when the spec declares it `type: string`
   (still valid); a custom action that assigned an item only because the transit could not
   (`assign` beside the transit) may become the transit with `relation` in its payload.
7. With `uploadable` in `packages`, `screens` wraps the routes in `FileFieldProvider` whatever
   the fields of the entities (the bodies of the actions may take files): add `file-field`
   (`manage.py bazis_front add file-field`) if the product has not. A file chosen in a form is
   shown from the browser until the form is saved, not read back.
8. A public form with files (a custom screen with its own upload, or the photos sent as
   base64 in the body): take `photos: list[UploadedFileId]` in the body, turn the anonymous
   uploads on (bazis-uploadable 2.8, `BAZIS_FILE_UPLOAD_ANONYMOUS` with
   `BAZIS_FILE_UPLOAD_MAX_SIZE`), attach them with `FileUpload.objects.anonymous`, export the
   contract and list the property in `fields` of the form (S057 until the uploads are on).
9. A body typed by a `Literal` gets S058: type it by the `TextChoices` of the model, whose
   labels the form offers with bazis 2.21.
