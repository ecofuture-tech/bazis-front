# bazis-front 0.2.0

This version was never published: its changes are part of [0.3.0](0.3.0.md), and a product
of 0.1.0 migrates with the one recipe there ("Migrating from 0.1.0"). The notes stay as the
history of what 0.2.0 changed.

The screens of a product get their design, new parts and a visual check. It requires
`bazis>=2.16.0`, as 0.1.0 did. Nothing of the API of the copies is removed: the changes
of the components are new props and new defaults.

## What is new

- **The layers of the design.** `theme.yaml` names a palette (`indigo`, `slate`, `ocean`,
  `forest`, `ember`, `plum`), a pair of fonts (`system`, `inter`, `geist`, `manrope`,
  `ibm-plex`, `source-serif-display` or a family of Google Fonts), a shape (`soft`,
  `round`, `sharp`) and a surface (`bordered`, `flat`, `elevated`), merged over those of
  the preset and under `tokens.json`, which becomes optional. A design that names no layer
  keeps its look. `mode` (`light`, `dark`), `composition.list_phone` and the icons of the
  entities are part of it. `bazis_front design` also writes `src/bazis/generated/fonts.css`
  from the `@fontsource` packages of the template.
- **The contract labels its data.** `contract.json` (format 5) gives every field its
  `title` and every resource its `label`, the attributes that label an item. The
  components show the titles, never a raw name or an id: a card without a name is headed by
  its label fields, a status by its name, a boolean filter by its chips. `product.yaml`
  gets `display` of a field (`plain`, `currency`, `unit`, `bar`, `phone`) and `label` of an
  entity.
- **New components.** `dashboard` (the tiles of `stat-card`, the quick links, the charts,
  the last items), `charts` (recharts), `date-picker` (react-day-picker, for the dates of the
  forms and the filters), `resource-board` with `board-dnd` (a column per status of a
  workflow of bazis-statusy; its cards move only with `move`, by their menu or by drag and
  drop with @dnd-kit), `resource-calendar` (a month or a week by a date field, the list of
  the selected day), `empty-state`, and the shadcn/ui components on Radix `select`, `tabs`,
  `dropdown-menu`, `tooltip`, `checkbox`, `switch`, `avatar`, `separator`, `scroll-area`
  and `calendar`. The libraries of charts, dates and drag and drop are loaded with their own
  chunks.
- **The list and the card.** `ResourceList` shows cards at the width of a phone, selects
  rows, runs the bulk actions of a list on them (a request per row; the rows that the backend
  refuses stay selected with the reason), hides columns and changes the density in its
  view, and filters and sorts with the select of Radix. `ResourceCard` has tabs. A screen
  links back to the screen that opened it (`Screen` with `parent`).
- **The primitives of the screens.** Besides `list`, `card` and `form`, the screen specs have
  `dashboard`, `board` and `calendar`, `card.tabs` and `list.bulk`, the steps `select`,
  `bulk` and `move` of the scenarios and their expectations of a tile and a day, with the
  helpers of Playwright that run them. `init` writes the home of the specs as a dashboard.
- **The visual check.** `manage.py bazis_front shots` generates `e2e/generated/shots.ts`
  from the specs, takes the screenshots of every screen at the width of a desktop and of a
  phone in each color mode, and reports in `.scratch/shots/report.json` what they render:
  V001-V013 (a screen not shot, a page wider than a phone, a raw name or a UUID shown, the
  contrast as rendered, a light surface in the dark mode, an empty state without its action,
  a control too small to touch, a text cut, an error of the page, of its console or of a
  request to the API). A list is also shot with its empty state and its create form open.
- **The loading of a screen.** `lazyScreen` shows the loading state as soon as a screen is
  opened, not after its chunk is loaded with the previous screen still shown.
- **New codes.** The specs: S018, S019 (the titles and the labels), S020 and P031 (`display`
  and `label`), S021-S025 (the dashboard, the board, the calendar, the tabs, the bulk
  actions), S026 (the home of a product with entities), D010 and D011 (the dark mode, the
  icons), D012 (a layer of the design hidden by `tokens.json`). The system checks: `front.W008` (a stale `shots.ts`), `front.W009` (the errors of
  the last screenshots, or the screenshots not taken).
